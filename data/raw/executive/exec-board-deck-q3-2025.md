# Board Meeting Deck — Q3 2025

## Executive Summary  
Q3 2025 marks a pivotal quarter for PKC Technologies as we consolidate our AI‑driven product suite and expand the Austin satellite office. Revenue grew **₹1,240 crore**, up **18 % YoY**, driven by the launch of **PKC‑Insight** (enterprise analytics) and the **EdgeAI** platform for IoT devices. Net profit reached **₹210 crore**, reflecting a **22 % margin improvement** after disciplined cost management. The board’s focus remains on scaling the Bengaluru R&D hub, deepening US market penetration, and strengthening our talent pipeline ahead of the upcoming fiscal year.

## Financial Performance  

| Metric | Q3 2025 | Q3 2024 | YoY Δ |
|--------|--------|--------|------|
| Revenue | **₹1,240 crore** | ₹1,050 crore | +18 % |
| Gross Profit | ₹720 crore | ₹610 crore | +18 % |
| EBITDA | ₹340 crore | ₹280 crore | +21 % |
| Net Profit | **₹210 crore** | ₹170 crore | +24 % |
| Operating Expenses | ₹530 crore | ₹500 crore | +6 % |

Key drivers:  
- **PKC‑Insight** contributed **₹180 crore** in new ARR.  
- US contracts secured in Q2 2025 added **₹45 crore** incremental revenue.  
- Cloud infrastructure optimization saved **₹12 crore** YoY.

## Operational Highlights  

- **Product Launches:** PKC‑Insight (Mar 2025) and EdgeAI v2.1 (Jun 2025). Both achieved >90 % SLA compliance.  
- **Customer Wins:** Tata Motors (₹30 crore), Reliance Retail (₹22 crore), and a multi‑year AI‑ops contract with a Fortune 500 US retailer (₹45 crore).  
- **Infrastructure:** Completed Phase‑II of the Bengaluru data centre, adding 150 TB of storage and 30 % compute capacity.  
- **Compliance:** ISO 27001 recertified (valid till Dec 2027); GDPR readiness audit passed with no major findings.

## Talent & HR  

- **Headcount:** 1,820 employees (Bengaluru 1,560; Austin 260).  
- **Attrition:** 6.2 % (Q3) – down from 8.1 % YoY, thanks to the new “PKC‑Growth Path” program.  
- **Hiring:** 120 engineers onboarded in Q3, focusing on AI‑research, data‑science, and cloud‑ops.  
- **Key Appointments:**  
  - **Ananya Rao**, Chief Product Officer (effective 1 May 2025).  
  - **Rohit Mehta**, Head of US Business Development (effective 15 Jun 2025).  

Employee engagement scores rose to **84 %** in the quarterly pulse survey (benchmark 80 %).  

## Risk & Compliance  

- **Regulatory:** Monitoring upcoming AI‑ethics guidelines from the Ministry of Electronics & IT; compliance roadmap drafted.  
- **Currency Exposure:** USD‑INR volatility hedged for 70 % of US contracts via forward contracts (average hedge rate ₹82.5/USD).  
- **Cybersecurity:** No material incidents; ongoing penetration testing scheduled for Sep 2025.  

## Outlook & Strategic Initiatives  

- **Revenue Target FY 2026:** **₹5,200 crore** (≈ ₹1,300 crore per quarter).  
- **Product Roadmap:** Release PKC‑Vision (computer‑vision suite) by Dec 2025; initiate pilot for AI‑driven supply‑chain optimizer Q1 2026.  
- **Geographic Expansion:** Establish a sales liaison office in Singapore (budget **₹8 crore**) to capture Southeast Asian demand.  
- **Capital Expenditure:** Allocate **₹150 crore** for AI‑research labs and talent development scholarships (incl. 30 % for women‑in‑tech initiatives).

3. **Sanction hiring plan** for 250 additional engineers across Bengaluru and Austin.
