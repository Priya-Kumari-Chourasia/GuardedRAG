# Due Diligence Notes — Project Falcon

## Overview  

Project Falcon is a strategic AI‑driven analytics platform aimed at the mid‑market retail sector. Initiated on **12 January 2024**, the project is currently in the prototype validation stage, with a target commercial launch slated for **15 October 2024** (coinciding with the festive Diwali sales window). The core team comprises 22 engineers, 4 data scientists, and 3 product managers based in Bengaluru, supported by a satellite liaison team of 5 staff in Austin, USA.

## Legal & Compliance  

- **IP Ownership:** All code and model artefacts are assigned to PKC Technologies under the “Employee Invention Assignment” clause (effective 1 Jan 2023).  
- **Data Privacy:** The platform processes anonymised POS data; compliance with India’s Personal Data Protection Bill (draft) and GDPR (for US‑based data) has been validated by external counsel, Karan Sharma & Associates.  
- **Regulatory Approvals:** No sector‑specific licences required; however, a **Form‑30** filing with the Ministry of Electronics & Information Technology is scheduled for **30 April 2024**.

## Technical Assessment  

- **Architecture:** Micro‑services on Kubernetes (EKS in US, GKE in India).  
- **Model Stack:** Transformer‑based demand forecasting (BERT‑Lite) with 96 % accuracy on pilot datasets (RetailCo, Bangalore).  
- **Scalability:** Benchmarked to handle 1.2 M transactions per hour with 99.9 % uptime SLA.  
- **Security:** End‑to‑end encryption, regular penetration testing (last on **15 March 2024**).  

## Risks & Mitigations  

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| Cloud cost overruns (US‑India data transfer) | Medium | High | Implement tiered data caching; negotiate volume discounts with AWS. |
| Talent attrition post‑Diwali (₹30 L bonus period) | Low | Medium | Introduce retention bonus tied to launch milestones. |
| Regulatory change (PDP Bill) | Medium | High | Ongoing monitoring by legal team; design data pipelines for rapid policy adaptation. |
