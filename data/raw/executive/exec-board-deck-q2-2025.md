# Board Meeting Deck — Q2 2025

## Executive Summary  
Q2 2025 (April – June) marked a pivotal growth phase for PKC Technologies. Revenue accelerated to **₹12.6 crore**, a **28 % YoY** increase, driven by the launch of **PKC‑Insight**, our AI‑driven analytics platform for mid‑market enterprises. Net profit stood at **₹2.1 crore**, reflecting improved operating leverage and tighter cost controls. The board’s focus this quarter is on scaling the Austin satellite office, deepening our partnership ecosystem, and ensuring compliance with upcoming RBI data‑localisation mandates.

## Financial Highlights  

| Metric | Q2 2025 | Q2 2024 | YoY Δ |
|--------|--------|--------|------|
| Revenue | **₹12.6 crore** | ₹9.8 crore | +28 % |
| Gross Margin | 62 % | 58 % | +4 pts |
| EBITDA | **₹3.4 crore** | ₹2.5 crore | +36 % |
| Net Profit | **₹2.1 crore** | ₹1.6 crore | +31 % |
| Operating Expenses | **₹4.9 crore** | ₹5.2 crore | –5 % |
| Cash & Cash Equivalents (EoQ) | **₹6.8 crore** | ₹5.5 crore | +24 % |

Key drivers: 1) **PKC‑Insight** contributed **₹4.2 crore** (33 % of total revenue). 2) Cloud infrastructure cost optimisation saved **₹0.6 crore** YoY. 3) New contracts with **Tata Consultancy Services** and **Infosys** added **₹2.5 crore** in ARR.

## Operational Highlights  

- **Product Launch:** PKC‑Insight went GA on 12 May 2025. Early adopters report a 22 % reduction in data‑processing latency.  
- **Austin Office Expansion:** Added 12 engineers (total headcount = 48). Lease for new 5,000 sq ft space at **+91‑80‑1234‑5678** (Bengaluru HQ) and **+1‑512‑555‑0198** (Austin) signed on 3 June.  
- **Customer Success:** Net promoter score (NPS) rose to **71**, up from 64 in Q1.  
- **Compliance:** Completed RBI‑mandated data‑localisation audit ahead of the 15 August deadline.

## R&D & Product Updates  

- **AI Core Team:** Expanded to 35 members; 60 % of the team now holds PhDs in machine learning.  
- **Roadmap:** Q3 2025 will see the beta release of **PKC‑Voice**, an AI‑powered speech analytics suite, slated for 20 July.  
- **Patents:** Filed 4 new patents (total portfolio = 27).  

## Market & Sales  

- **Geography:** Domestic revenue grew 31 % YoY; US revenue (Austin) reached **₹1.1 crore**, a 45 % increase.  
- **Channel Strategy:** Launched a reseller program with **Tech Mahindra** and **Wipro**, targeting the SMB segment.  
- **Key Wins:** Secured a 3‑year contract with **Reliance Industries** worth **₹3.8 crore** (signed on 28 May).  

## Human Resources  

- **Headcount:** 212 employees (Bengaluru = 164, Austin = 48).  
- **Attrition:** 6 % (Q2) – down from 9 % in Q1, thanks to the new employee wellness program introduced on **15 June** (aligned with **Independence Day** celebrations).  
- **Compensation:** Average salary uplift of **₹3.5 L** per employee, reflecting market adjustments.  

## Risk & Compliance  

- **Data Security:** Implemented ISO 27001 certification (effective 1 July).  
- **Regulatory:** Ongoing monitoring of the Personal Data Protection Bill; mitigation plan ready for the **Diwali** (15 Nov) compliance window.  

## Outlook & Guidance (Q3 2025)  

- Revenue target: **₹14.5 crore** (+15 % QoQ).  
- EBITDA margin: 24 % (maintain).  
- Capital expenditure: **₹1.2 crore** for cloud infrastructure and Austin office fit‑out.  

## Action Items for the Board  

1. Approve **₹2.5 crore** additional funding for PKC‑Voice development (deadline: 30 June).  
2. Endorse the **Strategic Alliance** with **Microsoft Azure India** for joint AI services (signed on 5 June).  
3. Review and ratify the **Data Governance Framework** ahead of the RBI deadline (review meeting on 12 July).
