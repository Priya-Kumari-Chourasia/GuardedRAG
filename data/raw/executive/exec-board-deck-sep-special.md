# Board Meeting Deck — September 2025 Special Session

## Overview  
The September 2025 Special Session addresses the accelerated restructuring of PKC Technologies’ AI‑Platform division. The decision follows the post‑Diwali market slowdown and the upcoming fiscal year‑end (31 March 2026). The board authorises a phased realignment that will streamline product road‑maps, consolidate R&D spend, and reposition our go‑to‑market strategy for the next growth cycle.

## Strategic Decision  
- **Consolidate AI‑Platform** into two core product lines: **PKC Vision** (computer‑vision) and **PKC Insight** (analytics).  
- **Close** the under‑performing **PKC Edge** sub‑team (responsible for low‑margin edge‑device SDKs).  
- **Re‑allocate** resources to the **Data‑Science Lab** in Bengaluru and the **Customer Success Hub** in Austin.  

The board approved a **₹12.4 crore** one‑time restructuring cost, funded from the FY 2025 discretionary reserve.

## Financial Impact  
| Item | FY 2025 (Actual) | FY 2026 (Projected) |
|------|------------------|---------------------|
| Revenue – AI‑Platform | ₹78.6 crore | ₹92.3 crore (↑ 17.5 %) |
| Operating Expense – AI‑Platform | ₹65.1 crore | ₹58.7 crore (‑ 9.8 %) |
| Net Contribution | ₹13.5 crore | ₹33.6 crore (↑ 149 %) |
| One‑time Restructuring Cost | — | **₹12.4 crore** |
| Expected FY 2026 EBITDA uplift | — | **₹21.1 crore** |

The net effect is an **₹21.1 crore** EBITDA uplift for FY 2026, offset by the one‑time cost, delivering a **₹8.7 crore** net gain by year‑end.

## Affected Teams & Resources  
- **PKC Edge** (Bengaluru) – 42 engineers, 8 QA, 3 product managers – to be redeployed or offered voluntary exit.  
- **Data‑Science Lab** – +15 senior data scientists (new hires) to accelerate PKC Insight.  
- **Customer Success Hub** (Austin) – +5 account managers to support expanded enterprise contracts.  
- **Finance & Legal** – tasked with severance budgeting (₹1.2 crore) and IP transfer agreements.

## Confidential Rationale (Board‑Only)  
Public communications framed the move as “optimising for market demand.” Internally, the board concluded that **PKC Edge** suffered from:  

1. **Strategic mis‑fit** – its technology stack (TensorFlow Lite v1) is obsolete relative to the company‑wide migration to **PyTorch 2.0**.  
2. **Margin erosion** – average gross margin of **28 %**, well below the 45 % target for AI‑Platform.  
3. **Talent attrition risk** – 38 % of Edge engineers received external offers in Q2 2025, threatening knowledge loss.  

The confidential analysis also revealed a **latent IP overlap** with a pending patent filing by a competitor, raising potential litigation exposure. Consolidating IP under PKC Vision and Insight mitigates this risk and strengthens our defensive portfolio.

## Risks & Mitigations  
- **Talent loss** – Offer ₹5 lakh signing bonus for critical redeployments; engage external recruiters for niche roles.  
- **Customer churn** – Deploy dedicated account managers (Austin) within 30 days of transition.  
- **Regulatory compliance** – Legal team to audit all severance and IP transfer agreements by 25 Sep 2025.
