# 2026 Strategic Priorities Memo

## Overview  

PKC Technologies (PKC) is positioned to capitalize on the rapid adoption of generative AI across enterprise and consumer segments in FY‑2026‑27. This memo outlines the strategic priorities that will drive a **₹1,250 crore** revenue target, deepen our AI‑core IP, and expand our presence in North America while maintaining operational excellence in Bengaluru.

## Financial Outlook  

| Metric (FY‑26‑27) | Target |
|-------------------|--------|
| Total Revenue | **₹1,250 crore** (↑ 22% YoY) |
| Gross Margin | **68%** |
| R&D Spend | **₹210 crore** (≈ 17% of revenue) |
| Sales & Marketing | **₹150 crore** |
| Net Profit | **₹95 crore** (≈ 7.6% margin) |

Revenue mix: **₹720 crore** from AI‑platform subscriptions, **₹380 crore** from custom solutions, **₹150 crore** from licensing of proprietary models. The North America satellite office in Austin will contribute **₹120 crore** by FY‑27, driven by enterprise contracts secured during Q3‑Q4.

## Talent & Culture  

- **Headcount**: Grow from 1,200 to **1,450** employees by 31 Mar 2027 (≈ 21% increase).  
- **Hiring Plan**: Add 180 AI‑research engineers, 120 solution architects, and 50 sales specialists.  
- **Compensation**: Senior AI Engineer salary band **₹30‑45 LPA**; Principal Scientist **₹55‑70 LPA**; Sales Director **₹40‑55 LPA** plus performance bonus.  
- **Retention**: Introduce a “PKC Innovation Grant” of **₹5‑10 L** per employee for internal AI projects, to be announced on **15 Jan 2026** (post‑Republic Day).  

All new hires will undergo the **PKC Onboarding Week** (first week of June) aligned with the **Monsoon Festival** (regional cultural observance) to foster cross‑office bonding.

## Product Roadmap  

1. **PKC‑Core 3.0** (launch **1 Oct 2026**): next‑gen multimodal model with 10× inference speed improvement.  
2. **EdgeAI Suite** (beta **15 Feb 2027**): lightweight models for IoT devices, targeting the automotive and agritech sectors.  
3. **Compliance‑AI** (GA release **30 Apr 2027**): built‑in GDPR‑India and data‑sovereignty modules for regulated industries.  

Each product line will be supported by a dedicated **₹30‑₹45 crore** go‑to‑market budget, with quarterly OKRs tracked via the PKC Dashboard.

## Market Expansion  

- **North America**: Secure three marquee enterprise contracts (banking, healthcare, logistics) by **31 Dec 2026**.  
- **APAC**: Pilot EdgeAI Suite in Singapore and Tokyo, leveraging existing partnerships with **₹12 crore** joint‑venture funds.  
- **India**: Deepen penetration in the BFSI sector during **Diwali** (Oct 2026) with targeted promotional bundles.

## Risk & Mitigation  

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Talent attrition in AI research | Introduce equity‑linked “AI‑Partner” program; quarterly salary reviews aligned with market index. |
| Data‑privacy regulatory changes | Deploy Compliance‑AI early; maintain a legal advisory board (lead: Ms. Ananya Rao, Senior Counsel). |
| Currency volatility (INR/USD) | Hedge 60% of North America revenue via forward contracts; maintain a **₹5 crore** contingency reserve. |

## Governance & KPIs  

- **Executive Steering Committee** meets monthly (first Thursday) – next meeting **07 May 2026** (post‑Eid).  
- KPI Dashboard includes: Revenue run‑rate, R&D milestone completion %, employee NPS, and carbon‑footprint reduction (target **15%** by FY‑27).  
- Quarterly Business Reviews (QBR) will be conducted on **15 July**, **15 Oct**, **15 Jan**, and **15 Apr**.
