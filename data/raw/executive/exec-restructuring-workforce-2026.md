# Workforce Planning — 2026

## 1. Executive Summary  

PKC Technologies is undertaking a calibrated workforce restructuring to align head‑count with the 2026 product‑roadmap and to improve EBITDA margins from 12 % to 18 %. The plan will reduce total head‑count by **4.2 % (≈ 210 employees)** while reallocating talent to high‑growth AI‑ops and Cloud‑Native divisions. Expected net cost saving: **₹15.8 crore** in FY‑27, with a modest up‑skill investment of **₹3.2 crore**.

---

## 3. Strategic Objectives  

1. **Focus on AI‑ops & Edge‑AI** – increase engineering head‑count in these verticals by **12 %**.  
2. **Consolidate non‑core platforms** – phase‑out legacy SaaS modules, shifting support to a shared services hub in Bengaluru.  
3. **Enhance diversity** – achieve **30 %** women representation in senior technical roles by FY‑28.  
4. **Maintain compliance** with Indian labour laws and the **Industrial Relations (Amendment) Act, 2020**.

---

## 4. Restructuring Actions  

| Action | Impact | Timeline | Cost / Saving |
|--------|--------|----------|---------------|
| Voluntary Separation Scheme (VSS) – 150 employees (mid‑senior) | Reduces head‑count by 5.8 % | 01 Jun 2026 – 30 Jun 2026 | Saving ₹9.4 crore |
| Role‑based redeployment – 60 engineers to AI‑ops | Upskilling cost ₹1.2 crore | 01 Jul 2026 – 31 Oct 2026 | No net cost |
| Closure of Bengaluru “Legacy Platform” unit – 30 staff | Consolidation to shared services | 01 Nov 2026 – 31 Dec 2026 | Saving ₹3.6 crore |
| New hires – 45 AI‑ops specialists (₹25 L each) | Supports growth target | 01 Jan 2027 – 31 Mar 2027 | Cost ₹11.3 crore |
| Diversity leadership program – 20 women senior hires | Improves gender ratio | 01 Feb 2027 – 30 Jun 2027 | Cost ₹0.8 crore |

Total net saving after up‑skill and hiring outlay: **₹15.8 crore**.

---

## 5. Financial Impact  

- **Salary budget reduction:** ₹68.2 crore (≈ 14 % of total).  
- **One‑time VSS payout:** ₹9.4 crore.  
- **Training & recruitment outlay:** ₹3.2 crore.  
- **Projected FY‑27 EBITDA uplift:** +6.2 % (₹29.5 crore).  

All figures are in **₹** and expressed using lakh/crore notation.
