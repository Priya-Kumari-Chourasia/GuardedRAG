# M&A Exploration — Potential Acquisition Targets

## Executive Summary  
PKC Technologies is evaluating three mid‑market software firms as potential acquisition targets to accelerate our AI‑driven product suite and expand our presence in the North‑East Indian market. The targets collectively generate ₹1,845 crore in annual revenue, with an estimated combined EBITDA margin of 22 %. Preliminary valuation suggests a total deal size of ₹4.2 crore (₹420 crore) inclusive of earn‑outs and integration costs. The proposed timeline aligns with the fiscal year‑end (31 March 2025) and avoids major Indian holidays such as Republic Day (26 Jan) and Diwali (15 Nov 2024).

## Target Overview  

| # | Company | HQ (City, State) | Core Offering | FY‑24 Revenue | FY‑24 EBITDA | Valuation (₹ crore) |
|---|---------|------------------|---------------|--------------|--------------|----------------------|
| 1 | **DataMitra Solutions** | Hyderabad, Telangana | Cloud‑based data‑analytics platform for retail | ₹642 crore | ₹141 crore | ₹150 |
| 2 | **NeuroEdge Labs** | Pune, Maharashtra | Edge‑AI inference engine for IoT devices | ₹398 crore | ₹88 crore | ₹110 |
| 3 | **SutraSoft Pvt. Ltd.** | Jaipur, Rajasthan | Low‑code enterprise app builder | ₹805 crore | ₹176 crore | ₹160 |

All three entities are privately held, with founders still in operational roles, and have clean balance sheets (net debt < ₹10 crore each).

## Financial Assessment  

- **Aggregate Revenue:** ₹1,845 crore (FY‑24) – 18 % YoY growth driven by SaaS subscriptions.  
- **EBITDA Margin:** 22 % (₹405 crore) – above industry average of 18 %.  
- **Purchase Price Multiples:** 9.5× EBITDA (average) – comparable to recent Indian SaaS deals (e.g., Zoho acquisition of a niche AI startup at 9× EBITDA, Jan 2024).  
- **Integration Cost Estimate:** ₹12 crore (technology harmonisation, HR onboarding, legal).  
- **Projected Synergy:** ₹30 crore incremental EBITDA within 24 months from cross‑selling PKC’s AI APIs to target customers and consolidating data‑centres in Bengaluru and Austin.

## Strategic Fit  

1. **Product Complementarity** – DataMitra’s analytics engine can be embedded into PKC’s existing AI‑platform, creating a unified “Insight‑as‑a‑Service” offering.  
2. **Geographic Expansion** – NeuroEdge’s strong foothold in the IoT ecosystem of Maharashtra complements PKC’s upcoming smart‑city projects in Karnataka.  
3. **Talent Acquisition** – SutraSoft’s low‑code team (45 senior engineers) accelerates our roadmap for rapid prototyping tools slated for release Q3 2025.  
4. **Cross‑Border Leverage** – The Austin satellite office will serve as the integration hub for NeuroEdge’s US‑based OEM partners, leveraging existing GST‑compliant structures.
