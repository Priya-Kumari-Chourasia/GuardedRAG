# Org Restructuring Plan — Engineering Division

## 1. Executive Summary  

PKC Technologies is undertaking a strategic re‑organisation of its Engineering Division to accelerate product delivery, improve talent utilisation, and align resources with the FY‑2027 growth targets (₹1,250 crore revenue). The plan consolidates overlapping teams, introduces a Platform Services layer, and creates a dedicated AI‑Innovation Hub in Bengaluru. The restructuring will be executed between 01‑02‑2025 and 31‑05‑2025, with minimal disruption to ongoing projects.

## 3. Proposed Structure  

1. **Platform Services Group** (Head: **Ananya Rao**, +91‑80‑2345 6789) – merges Back‑end, DevOps, and Cloud Infra into a single service‑oriented team (120 FTE).  
2. **Product Engineering Pods** – three cross‑functional pods (Consumer, Enterprise, Edge) each with 45 FTE (Front‑end, QA, and selected Back‑end resources).  
3. **AI‑Innovation Hub** – expanded to 80 FTE, reporting to the CTO, focusing on generative AI and industry‑specific models.  
4. **Quality Excellence Centre** – relocated to Bengaluru (20 FTE) to co‑locate with Platform Services, improving defect‑turnaround time.  

**Headcount Impact:** Net reduction of 15 FTE (≈ 5 %); 10 FTE will be offered internal mobility, 5 FTE will receive voluntary separation packages (average ₹12 L per employee).  

**Financial Impact:**  
- Annual salary savings: **₹6.3 crore**.  
- Consolidated tooling licences: **₹1.2 crore** reduction.  
- One‑time transition cost (severance, re‑skilling): **₹2.5 crore**.  
- Net FY‑2025 EBITDA uplift: **₹5.0 crore**.
