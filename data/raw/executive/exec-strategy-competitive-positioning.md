# Competitive Positioning Memo — Executive Summary

## Overview  

PKC Technologies (PKC) continues to solidify its position as a leading AI‑driven software provider in the Indian and North‑American markets. FY 2025‑26 (April 2025 – March 2026) revenue is projected at **₹1,842 crore**, a **28 % YoY growth** driven by the Enterprise Automation Suite (EAS) and the newly launched Generative AI Platform (GAP). The Bengaluru headquarters employs **1,240** staff, while the Austin satellite office supports **180** engineers and sales personnel.  

## Market Landscape  

| Segment | Total Addressable Market (2026) | PKC Share | Key Competitors |
|---------|--------------------------------|-----------|-----------------|
| AI‑enabled ERP (India) | ₹5,600 crore | **12 %** | TCS, Infosys, Wipro |
| Generative AI SaaS (Global) | ₹9,300 crore | **3.4 %** | OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind |
| Edge‑AI for IoT (Asia‑Pac) | ₹2,150 crore | **7 %** | Huawei, Samsung, Bosch |

The Indian AI‑enabled ERP market is expected to expand at **22 % CAGR** through FY 2029, while global generative AI SaaS is forecast at **31 % CAGR**. PKC’s early mover advantage in low‑latency edge AI for manufacturing gives it a defensible niche in the Asia‑Pacific region.

## Competitive Advantages  

1. **Proprietary Knowledge Graph Engine** – reduces model inference latency by **38 %** versus open‑source alternatives.  
2. **Hybrid Cloud Architecture** – seamless deployment across on‑prem, AWS (US‑East‑1), and Azure India (Central) with a single‑pane management console.  
3. **Talent Pool** – 78 % of Bengaluru engineers hold PhDs or M.Tech in AI/ML; the Austin hub includes senior staff from NVIDIA and Microsoft.  

## Risks & Mitigations  

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| Talent attrition post‑Diwali (Oct 2025) | Medium | Introduce “Diwali Bonus Plus” of **₹1.5 L** per employee and flexible work‑from‑home policy. |
| US regulatory scrutiny on AI ethics (post‑June 2025) | High | Form an AI Ethics Committee chaired by Dr. Meera Nair (Chief Ethics Officer) and publish a compliance whitepaper by **15 July 2025**. |
| Currency volatility (INR/USD) | Low | Hedge 70 % of US‑derived revenue using forward contracts; maintain a cash buffer of **₹250 crore**. |

## Strategic Priorities FY 2025‑26  

1. **Scale GAP** – target **₹420 crore** ARR from North‑American SaaS contracts by Q4 2026.  
2. **Expand Edge‑AI** – launch pilot projects with two Tier‑1 automotive OEMs in Chennai and Pune by **31 March 2026**.  
3. **M&A Exploration** – identify a boutique AI‑analytics firm in Hyderabad with **₹85 crore** revenue for potential acquisition in H2 2026.  

## Key Milestones  

- **15 Jan 2026** – Release of GAP v2.0 with multimodal capabilities.  
- **26 Jan 2026 (Republic Day)** – Internal hackathon “AI for Bharat” – prize pool **₹10 L**.  
- **30 Apr 2026** – Launch of “PKC Edge‑AI Hub” at the India Tech Expo, Bengaluru.
