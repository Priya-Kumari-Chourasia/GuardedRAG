# Customer Account — Northwind Traders

## Overview  
Northwind Traders (NT) is a long‑standing B2B client of PKC Technologies, primarily consuming our AI‑driven supply‑chain optimization suite. The account was onboarded on **12 Feb 2022** and has since expanded to include predictive demand forecasting, automated procurement bots, and a custom analytics dashboard hosted on our Bengaluru data centre. NT’s fiscal year aligns with the Indian calendar (April – March).
