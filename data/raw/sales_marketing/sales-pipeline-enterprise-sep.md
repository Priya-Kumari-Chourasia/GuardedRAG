# Enterprise Deals Pipeline — September 2025

## 1. Executive Overview  

The September 2025 pipeline reflects a **₹12.6 crore** net‑new opportunity pool for PKC Technologies, driven primarily by enterprise AI‑automation contracts in the BFSI and manufacturing sectors. Compared with August, the pipeline grew **+18 %** YoY, with three deals now in the final negotiation stage and a projected close‑rate of **≈ 42 %** for the month.  

Key highlights:  

- **₹4.3 crore** in AI‑Ops platform licences for **Reliance Industries Ltd.** (expected sign‑off by 15 Sep).  
- **₹2.7 crore** multi‑year services agreement with **State Bank of India (SBI)** for fraud‑detection AI, slated for **30 Sep**.  
- New entry‑level deal with **Infosys Ltd.** for custom chatbot integration worth **₹68 L** (closing early Sep).
