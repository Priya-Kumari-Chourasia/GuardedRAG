# Sales Quota Sheet — Q4 2025

## Overview  

PKC Technologies — a leading AI‑driven software solutions provider headquartered in Bengaluru with a satellite office in Austin, USA—has finalized its Q4 2025 sales quota sheet. The quarter runs from 1 Oct 2025 to 31 Dec 2025 and aligns with the Indian fiscal calendar. The targets reflect the launch of **PKC‑Insight**, our next‑gen analytics platform, and the renewal cycle for existing enterprise contracts.

## Quarterly Revenue Target  

| Metric | Target | Rationale |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| **Total Q4 Revenue** | ₹ 12.5 crore | 22 % YoY growth driven by PKC‑Insight and upsell of AI‑Ops suite |
| **New Business Revenue** | ₹ 7.8 crore | 30 % of total from new logos in BFSI, Retail, and Healthcare |
| **Renewal & Expansion Revenue** | ₹ 4.7 crore | 60 % of existing ARR + 10 % cross‑sell |

## Incentive Structure  

| Achievement Level | Payout % of Base Salary | Additional Bonus |
|-------------------|------------------------|------------------|
| 80 % of quota | 10 % | None |
| 100 % of quota | 20 % | ₹ 2 L (fixed) |
| 120 % of quota | 30 % | ₹ 5 L + extra 0.5 % of over‑achievement revenue |
| 150 %+ of quota | 45 % | ₹ 10 L + 1 % of over‑achievement revenue |

Accelerators apply only to new‑business revenue; renewals are capped at 20 % of the base payout.
