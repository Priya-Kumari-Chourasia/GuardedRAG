# Customer Account — Global Retail Corp

## Overview  
PKC Technologies (PKC) has been serving Global Retail Corp (GRC) since Q2 2021, delivering AI‑driven inventory optimisation and omnichannel analytics. The partnership spans the Bengaluru headquarters and the Austin satellite office, enabling 24‑hour support across time zones. GRC’s FY 2025 spend with PKC is projected at **₹12.6 crore**, reflecting a 28 % YoY growth driven by the rollout of the new “Smart Shelf” module.

## Account History  
- **Q2 2021** – Signed a 3‑year Master Services Agreement (MSA) for ₹4.5 crore covering Predictive Demand Engine (PDE).  
- **Q4 2021** – Completed Phase 1 implementation across 150 stores; achieved 15 % reduction in stock‑outs.  
- **Q3 2022** – Added “Customer Sentiment Analyzer” (CSA) for ₹2.2 crore; pilot in 30 flagship outlets.  
- **Q1 2023** – Expanded to 300 stores; GRC renewed MSA for an additional ₹5.8 crore, extending support to 2026.  
- **Oct 2023 (Diwali)** – Launched “Smart Shelf” pilot in 20 stores; early results show 12 % uplift in average basket size.

## Product Portfolio for GRC  
- **Predictive Demand Engine (PDE)** – AI forecasting, integrated with SAP ECC.  
- **Customer Sentiment Analyzer (CSA)** – NLP‑based social listening, feeds into loyalty programmes.  
- **Smart Shelf** – Edge‑AI sensors + computer vision, real‑time shelf‑stock alerts.  
- **Retail Insights Dashboard** – Unified KPI view, mobile‑first UI, supports Hindi & English.
