# Sales Pipeline Report — Q3 2025

## Executive Summary  
Q3 2025 (01 Jul – 30 Sep) saw PKC Technologies’ sales pipeline expand to **₹12.6 crore** in total value, a **14 %** increase QoQ. The strongest growth came from the **Enterprise AI Platform** (EAP) vertical, driven by two marquee deals in the banking and logistics sectors. Net new qualified opportunities rose to **₹4.3 crore**, while the win‑rate improved to **28 %** (up from 22 % in Q2). The pipeline health score stands at **Green (≥ 75 % of target in ≥ 3 months)**, positioning us to exceed the FY 2025 revenue target of **₹150 crore**.
