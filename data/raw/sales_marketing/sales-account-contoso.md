# Customer Account — Contoso Manufacturing

## Overview  
PKC Technologies entered into a strategic partnership with **Contoso Manufacturing Ltd.** (CML) on 12‑Mar‑2023 to provide AI‑driven predictive maintenance and supply‑chain optimization solutions for their three major plants in Pune, Chennai, and Surat. The engagement is a 3‑year contract valued at **₹4.2 crore** per annum, with an annual escalation of 6 % tied to the Consumer Price Index.
