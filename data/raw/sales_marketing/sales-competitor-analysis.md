# Competitor Analysis — RAG Assistant Market

## Executive Summary  
PKC Technologies aims to capture a 12‑15 % share of the Indian Retrieval‑Augmented Generation (RAG) Assistant market by FY 2027. The market, valued at **₹4,800 crore** in FY 2024, is projected to grow at a CAGR of **28 %**, driven by enterprise digital transformation and government AI initiatives (e.g., Digital India). Our analysis identifies three primary competitors—**CognifyAI**, **NexGen Labs**, and **GlobalMind**—and highlights gaps in pricing, language support, and compliance that PKC can exploit.

## Market Overview  
- **Total addressable market (TAM):** ₹4,800 crore (FY 24) → **₹9,200 crore** by FY 27.  
- **Key verticals:** Banking & FinTech (30 %), Healthcare (22 %), E‑commerce (18 %), Public sector (15 %).  
- **Regulatory backdrop:** The AI‑Policy Draft released on 15 January 2025 mandates data localisation for all government‑related RAG deployments; compliance will be a decisive factor for public‑sector contracts.

## SWOT (PKC)  

- **Strengths:** Deep expertise in Indian language models; owned data‑centres in Bengaluru & Hyderabad; strong ties with RBI and Ministry of Health.  
- **Weaknesses:** Limited brand awareness outside South‑India; smaller sales force (30 reps vs. CognifyAI’s 80).  
- **Opportunities:** Upcoming government RFPs for “AI‑Enabled Citizen Services” (deadline 30 Oct 2025, Diwali season); rising demand for on‑prem RAG in banking due to RBI’s data‑localisation directive (effective 1 Apr 2025).  
- **Threats:** Aggressive pricing wars; potential entry of global giants leveraging existing cloud contracts.

## Recommendations  

1. **Launch “RAG‑Pro Enterprise”** by Q2 2025 with a **₹2,200 L** entry price, bundled with a 12‑month compliance audit service.  
2. **Accelerate regional sales** – hire 12 additional sales executives in Mumbai, Chennai, and Kolkata; target a **₹1,500 L** pipeline per rep by FY 26.  
3. **Leverage Indian holidays** for promotional campaigns:  
   - **Republic Day (26 Jan)** – “Freedom to Own Your Data” webinar series.  
   - **Diwali (Oct 2025)** – limited‑time discount of **10 %** on on‑prem licences.  
4. **Partner with local system integrators** (e.g., TCS, Wipro) to co‑sell on‑prem solutions, sharing revenue at **15 %**.  
5. **Invest in a dedicated compliance team** (₹3.5 crore FY 25 budget) to certify all deployments against the AI‑Policy Draft.
