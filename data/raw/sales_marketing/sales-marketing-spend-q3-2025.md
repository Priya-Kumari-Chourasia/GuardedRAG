# Marketing Spend Report — Q3 2025

## Executive Summary  
The Q3 2025 marketing spend for PKC Technologies (Bengaluru HQ, Austin satellite) totaled **₹4.28 crore**, representing a **7.3 %** increase over Q2 2025. The uplift was driven primarily by the launch of the “AI‑Edge 2025” product suite and a series of high‑visibility events aligned with key Indian holidays (Independence Day 15 Aug, Gandhi Jayanti 2 Oct). Overall campaign ROI improved to **₹12.6 revenue per ₹1 spent**, up from ₹10.8 in the prior quarter.

## Spend Overview (July – September 2025)  

| Category                | Q3 2025 Spend | % of Total | YoY Δ |
|-------------------------|--------------|------------|------|
| Digital Advertising     | ₹1.62 crore  | 37.8 %     | +9 % |
| Events & Sponsorships   | ₹1.04 crore  | 24.3 %     | +12 % |

| **Total**               | **₹4.28 crore** | 100 % | +7.3 % |

## Channel Breakdown  

### Digital Advertising  
- **Google Search & Display:** ₹78 L (CPC ₹45) – 1.8 M clicks, 3.2 % conversion.  
- **LinkedIn Sponsored Content:** ₹42 L – 1.1 M impressions, 0.9 % CTR.  
- **Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Carousel Ads:** ₹32 L – 2.4 M impressions, 1.1 % CTR.  

### Events & Sponsorships  
- **AI‑Edge 2025 Launch (Bengaluru, 18 Aug):** ₹55 L – 1,200 attendees, 85 % lead qualification.  
- **TechCrunch Disrupt (Austin, 27 Sep):** ₹30 L – 800 qualified leads, 12 % pipeline contribution.  
- **Independence Day Hackathon (Bengaluru, 15 Aug):** ₹19 L – 350 participants, 22 % conversion to trial.  

### Content & Thought‑Leadership  
- **Whitepaper “AI‑Driven Automation in Manufacturing”:** ₹22 L – 12 K downloads, 4.5 % MQL rate.  
- **Webinar series (4 sessions):** ₹18 L – 6.5 K registrants, 3.8 % conversion.  

### Public Relations  
- Press releases in **Economic Times**, **Business Standard**, and **TechRadar India** generated ₹45 L spend, yielding 18 media mentions and 3 × 10⁴ organic impressions.

## Performance Metrics  

- **Lead Generation:** 9,850 MQLs (↑ 15 % QoQ).  
- **Pipeline Contribution:** ₹5.38 crore (↑ 18 %).  
- **Cost per Lead (CPL):** ₹4,340 (↓ 6 %).  
- **Revenue Attribution:** ₹5.38 crore (directly linked to Q3 campaigns).  

## ROI & Attribution  

- **Overall ROI:** **₹12.6 revenue per ₹1 spent**.  
- **Digital Advertising ROI:** ₹13.4 per ₹1.  
- **Events ROI:** ₹11.2 per ₹1 (highest absolute revenue impact).  

## Recommendations for Q4 2025  

1. **Scale LinkedIn Sponsored Content** – increase budget by ₹12 L to capitalize on B2B lead quality.  
2. **Introduce Regional Micro‑Events** in Hyderabad and Pune during Diwali (Nov 2025) to sustain post‑Q3 momentum.  
3. **Invest in Marketing Automation** – allocate additional ₹10 L for AI‑driven lead scoring tools to further reduce CPL.  
4. **Strengthen PR in Tier‑2 cities** – target local tech publications to broaden brand awareness.
