# Regional Sales Targets — APAC

## Overview  
The APAC quota sheet outlines the FY2025‑26 sales targets for PKC Technologies’ regional sales force. The objectives are aligned with the corporate revenue goal of **₹1,250 crore** and are calibrated to reflect market potential across India, Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, Japan, and South Korea. Targets are expressed in lakh (L) and crore (Cr) rupees and are to be achieved by **31 March 2026**.

## Assumptions & Risks  

- **Currency Risk:** All APAC contracts are priced in INR; FX volatility is hedged quarterly.  
- **Regulatory Changes:** Anticipated data‑privacy amendments in Japan may delay closures by up to 4 weeks.  
- **Talent Availability:** Hiring for senior roles in Singapore and Japan is projected at 85% fill rate by Q2 2025.
