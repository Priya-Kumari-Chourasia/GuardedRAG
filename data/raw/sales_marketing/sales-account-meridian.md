# Customer Account — Meridian Logistics

## Overview  
Meridian Logistics (ML) is a mid‑size third‑party logistics provider headquartered in Mumbai, with regional hubs in Delhi and Chennai. The partnership with PKC Technologies began on **12 February 2022** and focuses on AI‑driven route optimisation, warehouse automation, and predictive demand analytics. The contract is a 3‑year agreement, renewable on an annual basis, with the current term ending **31 January 2025**.
