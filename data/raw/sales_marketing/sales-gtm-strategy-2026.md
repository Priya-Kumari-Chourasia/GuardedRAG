# 2026 Go-to-Market Strategy

## Executive Summary  
PKC Technologies aims to capture **₹1,250 crore** of new ARR by FY‑27 through a hybrid “AI‑First Enterprise” go‑to‑market (GTM) model. Leveraging our Bengaluru R&D hub and the Austin satellite for North‑American liaison, we will focus on three verticals—Banking & FinTech, Healthcare, and Smart Manufacturing—where AI‑driven automation can deliver ≥30 % cost savings. The strategy allocates **₹210 crore** (≈ 17 % of projected FY‑27 revenue) to sales‑marketing spend, targeting a 3.5 ×  ROI by Q4 2027.

## Market Landscape  
- **India AI Services market**: projected ₹9,800 crore by 2028 (CAGR ≈ 28 %).  
- **Key growth drivers**: Digital India initiatives, RBI’s AI‑enabled KYC, and the “Make in India” push for smart factories.  
- **Competitive set**: Infosys (₹2,400 crore AI revenue), TCS (₹3,100 crore), and emerging niche players such as Haptik and Niki.ai.  

## Target Segments & Personas  
| Segment | Revenue Potential (FY‑27) | Primary Persona | Pain Point |
|---------|---------------------------|----------------|------------|
| Banking & FinTech | ₹480 crore | Chief Data Officer | Fraud detection latency |
| Healthcare | ₹340 crore | Head of Clinical Analytics | Diagnostic accuracy |
| Smart Manufacturing | ₹430 crore | VP – Operations | Downtime & predictive maintenance |

## GTM Approach  
1. **Solution Bundles** – Pre‑packaged AI suites (e.g., “PKC FinGuard”, “PKC MediAI”, “PKC FactorySense”) priced at ₹12 L – ₹45 L per annum.  
2. **Channel Partnerships** – Tie‑ups with 12 Indian system integrators (e.g., Wipro, Tech Mahindra) and 4 US‑based VARs (e.g., Insight, CDW).  
3. **Regional Sales Pods** – Bengaluru (core), Hyderabad (banking), Pune (healthcare), and Austin (US‑based enterprise outreach). Each pod staffed with 1 senior sales lead, 2 solution engineers, and 1 partner manager.  

## Sales Enablement  
- **Training**: 120‑hour AI certification for all sales staff by 31 Mar 2026 (₹2 crore).  
- **Playbooks**: Vertical‑specific battle cards released on 15 Apr 2026.  
- **Incentives**: Tiered commission (10 %–18 %) plus quarterly “AI‑Champions” awards (₹5 L prize).  

## Marketing Mix  
- **Digital**: LinkedIn & Twitter ABM campaigns targeting 3,500 decision‑makers; budget ₹45 crore.  
- **Events**: Sponsor “AI Summit India” (13‑15 Jan 2026, Republic Day week) and host “PKC AI Hackathon” in Austin (22 Oct 2026, post‑Diwali).  
- **Content**: Publish 24 whitepapers, 12 case studies, and a quarterly “AI Insights” newsletter (₹8 crore).  
- **PR**: Secure 15 media placements in Economic Times, Business Standard, and TechCrunch India.

## KPIs & Milestones

- **Conversion**: 22 % win‑rate on demos (target).  
- **ARR Growth**: ₹250 crore Q1 2026 → ₹1,250 crore FY‑27.  
- **Partner‑Generated Revenue**: ≥₹300 crore by FY‑27.

## Risks & Mitigation  
- **Talent Attrition** – Introduce retention bonus (₹3 L per senior) and flexible hybrid work policy.  
- **Regulatory Changes** – Set up a compliance task‑force (led by Legal Head — Ananya Rao) to monitor RBI and FDA AI guidelines.  
- **Currency Fluctuation** – Hedge USD‑INR exposure for Austin contracts (₹1 USD ≈ ₹82).
