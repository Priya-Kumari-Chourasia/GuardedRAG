# Digital Ad Spend Breakdown — Q3 2025

## Overview  

For Q3 2025 (01 Jul 2025 – 30 Sep 2025), PKC Technologies allocated **₹4.78 crore** to digital advertising across its primary acquisition funnels. The spend was driven by the launch of the “AI‑Edge Suite” (released 12 Jul 2025) and a targeted push for enterprise contracts in the BFSI and Telecom verticals. The budget was split between paid search, programmatic display, LinkedIn Sponsored Content, and YouTube video ads, with a modest allocation for retargeting and brand‑awareness campaigns timed around Indian holidays (Independence Day 15 Aug and Gandhi Jayanti 2 Oct).

## Channel‑wise Allocation  

| Channel | Spend (₹) | % of Total | Key Campaigns | Avg. CPC / CPM |
|---------|-----------|------------|---------------|----------------|
| Google Search (Paid) | ₹1.62 crore | 34% | “AI‑Edge Suite – Intent Keywords”, “PKC Enterprise Demo” | ₹45 CPC |
| Programmatic Display (RTB) | ₹1.04 crore | 22% | “BFSI Vertical Targeting”, “Tech‑Savvy Professionals” | ₹210 CPM |
| LinkedIn Sponsored Content | ₹1.08 crore | 23% | “Thought‑Leadership Series”, “Enterprise Solutions Webinar” | ₹1,150 CPC |
| YouTube In‑Stream & Bumper | ₹0.68 crore | 14% | “AI‑Edge Demo Reel”, “Customer Success Stories” | ₹250 CPM |
| Retargeting (Meta & Google) | ₹0.36 crore | 7% | “Abandoned Demo Form”, “Product Tour Recall” | ₹55 CPC |

**Total Spend:** **₹4.78 crore**

**Overall CPL:** **₹3,860** (down 12% YoY).  

## ROI & Recommendations  

- **Revenue Attribution:** The Q3 digital spend generated **₹12.6 crore** in pipeline value, translating to an estimated **₹9.5 crore** in closed‑won revenue (≈ ₹2 crore incremental over Q3 2024).  
- **ROAS:** 2.6× overall; highest on LinkedIn (3.1×) due to high‑value enterprise leads.  
- **Optimization Points:**  
  1. **Shift 8% of display spend** to high‑perform BFSI programmatic sites (expected CPL reduction of ~₹800).  
  2. **Increase LinkedIn budget** by ₹12 L for Q4 to capture late‑year procurement cycles.  
  3. **Introduce a post‑Independence Day carousel ad** on Instagram (₹6 L) to boost brand recall among senior engineers.  

## Key Dates & Milestones  

- **12 Jul 2025:** AI‑Edge Suite launch – kickoff webinar (₹2.5 L production cost).  
- **15 Aug 2025:** Independence Day themed display burst – ₹45 L spend, 1.2 M impressions.  
- **02 Oct 2025:** Gandhi Jayanti retargeting push (planned for Q4).
