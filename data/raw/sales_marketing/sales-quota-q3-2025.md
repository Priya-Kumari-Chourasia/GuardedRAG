# Sales Quota Sheet — Q3 2025

## 1. Executive Overview  

PKC Technologies aims to sustain its rapid growth trajectory in FY 2025‑26 by capitalising on the Q3 (July – September 2025) market window. The sales quota sheet sets a **total revenue target of ₹120 crore** for the quarter, split across our two operating hubs – Bengaluru (India) and Austin (USA). The quota reflects a **12 % YoY uplift** over Q3 2024, driven by the launch of the next‑gen AI‑Assist platform and renewed enterprise contracts in the BFSI and telecom verticals.
