# Event Sponsorship Spend — 2025

## 1. Executive Summary  

PKC Technologies allocated **₹4.85 crore** for event sponsorships in FY 2025 (April 2024 – March 2025). The spend was spread across six high‑visibility industry gatherings in India and the United States, with a strategic focus on AI‑driven fintech, health‑tech, and cloud‑native platforms. The sponsorship mix (title, speaking, booth, and community‑partner tiers) delivered an estimated **₹12.3 crore** in incremental pipeline value and a **+18 %** lift in brand recall among target decision‑makers.

## 4. Budget Utilisation & Variance  

- **Planned Sponsorship Budget:** ₹4.85 crore  
- **Actual Spend:** ₹4.73 crore (₹12 L under) – savings realised through negotiated booth rates for the Austin event.  
- **Unspent Funds:** ₹12 L re‑allocated to Q4 digital amplification (LinkedIn & programmatic ads) to sustain momentum from the Diwali Tech Fest.
