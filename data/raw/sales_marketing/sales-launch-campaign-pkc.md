# Product Launch Campaign Plan — PKC Assistant

## Executive Summary  
The **Product Launch Campaign Plan – PKC Assistant** outlines a 6‑month go‑to‑market strategy aimed at capturing 12 % market share in the Indian enterprise AI‑assistant segment by Q4 FY 2025‑26. Leveraging PKC’s Bengaluru R&D hub and the Austin satellite office, the campaign will blend digital, events, and partner‑led tactics, with a total spend of **₹2.5 crore**.

## Campaign Objectives  
| # | Objective | Target | Measurement |
|---|-----------|--------|-------------|
| 1 | Generate qualified leads | 3,200 leads | Marketing‑qualified leads (MQL) tracked in HubSpot |
| 2 | Convert leads to paying customers | 480 new subscriptions | Closed‑won deals in Salesforce |
| 3 | Boost brand recall among CIOs | 70 % aided recall | Post‑campaign survey (N=1,200) |
| 4 | Achieve ARR uplift | ₹18.4 L (₹1.84 crore) incremental | Revenue reports Q4 FY 25‑26 |

## Target Market & Personas  
- **Enterprise CIOs (Tier‑1 & Tier‑2 cities)** – focus on banking, telecom, and FMCG.  
- **HR Heads** – seeking AI‑driven employee assistance.  
- **Product Managers** – interested in rapid prototyping with PKC Assistant APIs.

## Campaign Timeline (01 Oct 2025 – 31 Mar 2026)  

| Phase | Dates | Activities |
|-------|-------|------------|
| **Pre‑Launch** | 01 Oct 2025 – 15 Oct 2025 | Teaser videos, press release draft, internal training. |
| **Launch Event** | 20 Oct 2025 (Republic Day weekend) | Hybrid event in Bengaluru (BMR Convention Centre) + live stream to Austin office. |
| **Digital Push** | 21 Oct 2025 – 31 Dec 2025 | LinkedIn Sponsored Content, Google Search, programmatic display; 3 × webinars (15 Nov, 10 Dec, 5 Jan). |
| **Partner Enablement** | 01 Jan 2026 – 15 Feb 2026 | Co‑sell workshops with Accenture India & TCS; joint case studies. |
| **Field Activation** | 16 Feb 2026 – 28 Mar 2026 | Roadshows in Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai; participation in **Diwali Tech Expo** (15 Nov 2025) and **Independence Day Business Summit** (15 Aug 2026 – pre‑planned for FY 26). |
| **Close‑out & Review** | 01 Apr 2026 – 15 Apr 2026 | Data analysis, ROI calculation, lessons learned. |
