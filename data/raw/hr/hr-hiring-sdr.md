# Hiring Pipeline — Sales Development Rep

## Overview  
PKC Technologies is expanding its outbound sales engine to capture new enterprise accounts in the fintech and health‑tech verticals. The Sales Development Representative (SDR) role will be the first point of contact for inbound leads and will drive qualified pipeline for the Account Executives in Bengaluru and Austin. This hiring pipeline outlines the end‑to‑end process, timelines, and budget for onboarding **2 SDRs** (1 senior, 1 junior) by **15 September 2026**.

## Role Summary  
- **Title:** Sales Development Representative (SDR)  
- **Location:** Bengaluru (primary), with occasional remote support for Austin office  
- **Reporting to:** Head of Business Development – Ananya Rao  
- **Key Responsibilities:**  
  - Prospect 150–200 accounts weekly via LinkedIn, email, and cold‑call outreach.  
  - Qualify leads using BANT framework; achieve ≥ 30 qualified opportunities per month.  
  - Maintain CRM hygiene in HubSpot; update activity logs within 24 hrs.  
  - Collaborate with Marketing on ABM campaigns and provide feedback on lead quality.

## Interview Process Details  
1. **Phone Screen** – Verify communication skills, basic product knowledge, and availability.  
2. **Assessment** – 15‑question scenario covering objection handling and lead qualification.  
3. **Panel Interview** – Evaluate cultural fit, analytical thinking, and teamwork.  
4. **Leadership Interview** – Discuss career aspirations and alignment with PKC’s mission.
