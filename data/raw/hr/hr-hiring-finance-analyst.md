## 4. Interview Process & Evaluation  

1. **Phone Screen (15 min)** – Verify eligibility, notice period, and salary expectations.  
2. **Technical Case Study (1 hr)** – Candidate prepares a 5‑page variance analysis on a mock P&L (₹85 crore revenue, ₹32 crore OPEX). Scored on analytical rigor, Excel modeling, and storytelling.  
3. **Panel Interview (1.5 hr)** – Two finance peers + hiring manager assess domain knowledge, KPI familiarity (ARR, CAC, LTV), and cultural fit.  
4. **Leadership Round (45 min)** – HR Business Partner evaluates alignment with PKC values (Innovation, Integrity, Impact).  

Each interviewer submits a **Scorecard (1‑5)** on Technical, Analytical, Communication, and Culture. Minimum aggregate score of **3.5** required to proceed.
