# Compensation Bands — Sales

## 1. Purpose & Scope  

This document defines the compensation bands for all sales‑related roles at PKC Technologies (PKC). It applies to employees based in Bengaluru (head‑quarters) and Austin (satellite office) and supersedes any prior ad‑hoc arrangements. The bands are reviewed annually in line with the fiscal calendar (April – March).

## 2. Compensation Philosophy  

PKC follows a **base + variable** model to reward both market‑competitive salaries and performance‑driven outcomes. All figures are quoted in Indian Rupees (₹) using lakh (L) and crore (Cr) notation. Variable pay is tied to quarterly and annual sales targets, with a clear linkage to the company’s growth objectives.

*All salaries are gross annual figures and are payable in monthly installments.*

## 4. Variable Pay Mechanics  

- **Quarterly Incentive (QI):** 25 % of the annual variable target is paid at the end of each quarter, contingent on achieving ≥ 90 % of the quarterly quota.  
- **Annual Bonus (AB):** Remaining 75 % is paid in March, subject to meeting the FY target and a minimum company profitability of 12 % net margin.  
- **Accelerators:** Exceeding quota by 10 % triggers a 1.25× multiplier on the QI; 20 % excess triggers 1.5×.  

## 5. Performance Review Cycle  

- **Mid‑Year Review:** 15 July – aligns with the post‑Monsoon sales push.  
- **Year‑End Review:** 30 December – incorporates holiday season performance (including Diwali sales spike).  

Performance ratings (1‑5) directly affect the variable payout percentage (e.g., rating 5 = 100 % of target, rating 3 = 70 % of target).

## 6. Eligibility & Exceptions  

- New hires undergo a **probationary period of 3 months**; variable pay is prorated from the month of joining.  
- Employees on **extended leave** (e.g., maternity, sabbatical) receive base salary only; variable components are suspended.  

## 7. Payout Schedule & Taxation  

- All payouts are processed through the payroll system on the **last working day of each month**.  
- Variable components are subject to TDS as per the Income Tax Act, 1961.
