## Compliance & Notes  

1. **Holiday Pay:** Employees who worked on Ganesh Chaturthi received double pay for the day; no one was scheduled on Mahavir Jayanti.  
2. **Overtime Policy:** Overtime is compensated at ₹150 per hour for Bengaluru staff and ₹180 per hour for the Austin satellite, reflecting cost‑of‑living adjustments.  
3. **Taxation:** TDS calculations incorporated the new FY 2024‑25 surcharge of 4% for incomes above ₹25 L.  
4. **Audit Trail:** Payroll run ID PKC‑HR‑2025‑09‑30 is archived in the secure HRIS with access limited to HR, Finance, and Compliance leads.  
5. **Future Adjustments:** A salary revision cycle is slated for Q1 2026; projected average increase of 8% across the board.
