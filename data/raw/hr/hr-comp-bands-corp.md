# Compensation Bands — Corporate Functions

## 1. Overview  

PKC Technologies (PKC) aligns its corporate‑function compensation with market benchmarks, internal equity, and the company’s growth trajectory. The bands below apply to all non‑technical roles (Finance, Legal, People Operations, Marketing, and Administration) across the Bengaluru headquarters and the Austin satellite office (converted to INR for internal consistency). All figures are expressed in Indian Rupees using lakh (L) and crore (Cr) notation.

*Effective Date:* 1 April 2024  
*Next Review:* 1 April 2025 (or sooner if market conditions dictate)  

---

## 3. Benefits & Perks  

| Benefit | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Health Insurance** | Family floater covering up to ₹12 L per annum; includes mental‑health counseling. |
| **Retirement** | EPF contribution (12 % of basic) + optional NPS matching up to ₹1.5 L per year. |
| **Leave** | 21 calendar days annual leave + 12 days of national holidays (incl. Republic Day, Independence Day, Gandhi Jayanti) + 5 days of festive leave (Diwali, Navratri). |
| **Learning & Development** | Annual budget of ₹1.5 L per employee for certifications, conferences, or higher‑education courses. |
| **Mobility** | Bengaluru staff receive a ₹1 L yearly transport allowance; Austin staff receive a comparable INR‑equivalent allowance. |
| **Wellness** | On‑site gym (Bengaluru) and subsidised gym membership for remote staff; quarterly health‑check‑ups. |

## 4. Review Cycle & Process  

1. **Mid‑Year Calibration (July 2024):** Managers submit performance scores; HR validates against band caps.  
2. **Annual Compensation Committee (January 2025):** Finalise variable payouts and any band adjustments.  
3. **Communication:** Individual letters dispatched by 15 February 2025, with a Q&A session on 20 February 2025 (virtual).  

All changes are recorded in the HRIS and reflected in the payslip effective from the next payroll cycle (usually the 1st of the month).
