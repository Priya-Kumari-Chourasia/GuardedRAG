# Compensation Bands — Engineering

## 1. Purpose & Scope  
This document defines the salary bands for all engineering roles at PKC Technologies (PKC). It applies to staff based at the Bengaluru headquarters and the Austin satellite office (treated as an Indian‑registered entity for payroll). The bands are effective **1 April 2024** and will be reviewed annually.

## 2. Compensation Philosophy  
PKC follows a market‑aligned, performance‑driven pay structure. Base salary is benchmarked against the top quartile of Bengaluru’s AI/software market, with additional components for cost‑of‑living adjustments, statutory benefits, and discretionary bonuses tied to individual and company performance.

## 3. Salary Bands  

| Band | Designation | Experience | Base Salary (₹) | Variable Pay (% of Base) | Total Compensation (₹) |
|------|-------------|------------|-----------------|--------------------------|------------------------|
| **E1** | Junior Engineer | 0‑2 yr | ₹6.5 L – ₹9.0 L | 5 % | ₹6.8 L – ₹9.5 L |
| **E2** | Engineer | 2‑5 yr | ₹9.5 L – ₹13.5 L | 8 % | ₹10.3 L – ₹14.6 L |
| **E3** | Senior Engineer | 5‑9 yr | ₹14.0 L – ₹20.0 L | 12 % | ₹15.7 L – ₹22.4 L |
| **E4** | Lead Engineer | 9‑13 yr | ₹21.5 L – ₹28.5 L | 15 % | ₹24.7 L – ₹32.8 L |
| **E5** | Principal Engineer | 13 + yr | ₹30.0 L – ₹42.0 L | 20 % | ₹36.0 L – ₹50.4 L |

*All figures are **annual** and expressed in Indian Rupees using lakh (L) notation.*

### 3.1 Statutory & Fixed Allowances  
- **House Rent Allowance (HRA):** 40 % of base (for non‑metro employees) or 50 % (for metro).  
- **Provident Fund (PF):** 12 % of base contributed by employee & employer.  
- **Gratuity:** 4.81 % of base (as per the Payment of Gratuity Act).  
- **Medical & Insurance:** Company‑paid group health cover up to ₹12 L per employee + family.

## 4. Performance Bonus  
Bonuses are paid **post‑Diwali (typically 15 Nov 2024)** and are contingent on:  

1. **Individual KPI achievement** – up to 70 % of the variable component.  
2. **Company financial targets** – up to 30 % of the variable component.  

The maximum bonus for an E5 employee can reach **₹8.4 L** (20 % of ₹42 L base).

## 5. Salary Review Cycle  
- **Mid‑year market adjustment:** 1 July 2024 (aligned with the fiscal year).  
- **Annual performance review:** 1 January 2025, with salary increments effective 1 April 2025.  
- **Special adjustments:** Considered on **Republic Day (26 Jan)** and **Independence Day (15 Aug)** for exceptional talent retention.

## 6. Promotion Pathway  
Promotions are merit‑based and require:  

- Minimum **12 months** in the current band.  
- Demonstrated impact on at least **two** of the four PKC Engineering competency pillars (Innovation, Delivery, Leadership, Collaboration).  

A promotion triggers a **band‑jump salary uplift** of **10‑15 %** of the new band’s lower limit.
