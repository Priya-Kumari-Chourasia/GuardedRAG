# Hiring Pipeline — Senior Backend Engineer

## Role Overview  
**Position:** Senior Backend Engineer (Node.js / Go)  
**Location:** Bengaluru – PKC Technologies HQ (Whitefield, Bengaluru – 560066)  
**Reporting To:** Head of Platform Engineering – Ananya Rao  
**Team Size:** 8 engineers (4 junior, 2 mid‑level, 2 senior)  

The Senior Backend Engineer will own the design, development, and scaling of our core micro‑services platform that powers PKC’s AI‑driven SaaS suite. The role demands deep expertise in distributed systems, performance tuning, and cloud‑native deployments on AWS (us‑east‑1) and Azure (Central India).
