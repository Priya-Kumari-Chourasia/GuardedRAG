# Hiring Pipeline — Product Designer

## Overview  
PKC Technologies is expanding its product design team to accelerate the rollout of the next‑generation AI‑driven analytics platform (PKC‑Insight). The hiring pipeline outlined below details the end‑to‑end process for the **Product Designer (Mid‑Level)** role, aligned with our FY‑2027 recruitment targets.

## Role Summary  
- **Title:** Product Designer – Mid‑Level  
- **Location:** Bengaluru (HQ) – hybrid (3 days office, 2 days remote)  
- **Reporting to:** Amit Patel, Head of Product Design  
- **Key responsibilities:**  
  - Lead UI/UX design for PKC‑Insight dashboards and mobile companion app.  
  - Conduct user research, create wireframes, high‑fidelity prototypes, and design systems.  
  - Collaborate with product managers, data scientists, and front‑end engineers.  
  - Mentor junior designers and contribute to design‑ops best practices.

## Interview Process  

1. **HR Screening (30 min)** – Priya Sharma (HR Lead) assesses cultural fit, salary expectations, and notice period.  
2. **Portfolio Review (45 min)** – Amit Patel evaluates design depth, system thinking, and problem‑solving approach.  
3. **Design Challenge (48 hrs)** – Real‑world PKC‑Insight scenario; judged on usability, visual hierarchy, and documentation.  
4. **Panel Critique (60 min)** – Live critique with two senior designers and a product manager; focuses on rationale and iteration.  
5. **Leadership Interview (30 min)** – Discussion on long‑term vision, mentorship, and alignment with PKC’s AI ethics guidelines.
