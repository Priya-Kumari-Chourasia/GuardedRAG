# Q4 2025 Financial Report

## Executive Summary  
PKC Technologies delivered a robust performance in Q4 2025 (Oct 1 – Dec 31 2025), achieving a **₹12.6 crore** revenue, a **₹2.1 crore** increase over Q3, and an operating profit of **₹3.4 crore**. The quarter benefited from the successful launch of the **AI‑Assist 2.0** platform and the onboarding of three enterprise clients in the banking sector. Net cash balance at quarter‑end stood at **₹8.9 crore**, providing ample runway for FY 2026 initiatives.

## Revenue Breakdown  
| Segment | Q4 2025 | Q3 2025 | YoY Growth |
|---------|---------|---------|------------|
| AI SaaS (B2B) | ₹8.3 crore | ₹7.1 crore | +17% |
| Custom Software Services | ₹2.9 crore | ₹2.5 crore | +16% |
| Licensing & Maintenance | ₹1.4 crore | ₹1.2 crore | +17% |
| **Total Revenue** | **₹12.6 crore** | **₹10.8 crore** | **+16.7%** |

Key contracts signed:  
- **State Bank of India** – ₹3.2 crore (3‑year AI fraud detection module) – signed on 30 Oct 2025.  
- **Reliance Retail** – ₹1.5 crore (inventory optimisation AI) – signed on 12 Nov 2025.  

## Expense Summary  
- **Cost of Services**: ₹4.2 crore (incl. cloud hosting, third‑party APIs).  
- **R&D Expenditure**: ₹2.8 crore (30% of revenue, aligned with FY 2026 roadmap).  
- **Sales & Marketing**: ₹1.6 crore (including Diwali campaign – ₹45 L).  
- **General & Administrative**: ₹1.0 crore (office rent, utilities, headcount).  
- **Depreciation & Amortisation**: ₹0.4 crore.  

**Total Operating Expenses**: ₹10.0 crore, yielding an **EBITDA of ₹2.6 crore** and **Operating Profit of ₹3.4 crore** after accounting for other income (₹0.2 crore) and finance costs (₹0.4 crore).

## Headcount & HR Highlights  
- Total employees: **312** (Bengaluru = 274, Austin = 38).  
- New hires in Q4: **28** (AI engineers = 12, sales = 9, support = 7).  
- Attrition rate: **4.2%** (13 resignations, primarily for higher education).  
- **Employee Engagement Survey** conducted on 15 Dec 2025 – overall satisfaction **84%**.  

## Cash Flow & Balance Sheet Highlights  
- **Operating Cash Flow**: ₹3.1 crore.  
- **Investing Cash Flow**: –₹0.9 crore (capitalisation of new server rack, ₹55 L).  
- **Financing Cash Flow**: ₹0.0 crore (no new debt or equity).  
- **Cash & Cash Equivalents**: ₹8.9 crore.  
- **Total Debt**: ₹1.2 crore (short‑term working capital facility).  

## Outlook for FY 2026  
- Target revenue: **₹55 crore** (+22% YoY).  
- Planned R&D spend: **₹12 crore** (≈22% of revenue) focusing on **Generative AI for Healthcare** and **Edge‑AI for IoT**.  
- Expansion of Austin office: additional **10** engineers by Q2 2026.  
- Upcoming Indian holidays impacting staffing: **Republic Day (26 Jan 2026)** and **Mahashivratri (17 Feb 2026)** – pre‑planned staffing buffers in place.
