## 2. Scope of Services  

The Vendor shall provide end‑to‑end payroll processing for all PKC employees (approximately 1,850 staff as of 1 April 2025), including:

- Monthly salary computation, statutory deductions (PF, ESIC, Professional Tax) and tax filing (TDS).  
- Generation and electronic distribution of payslips in PDF format.  
- Management of off‑cycle payments (bonuses, reimbursements, leave encashment).  
- Annual compliance filing (Form 16, Form 24Q, PF returns) and assistance for audits.  
- Support desk (phone & email) available 9:00 am–6:00 pm IST, Monday to Friday, excluding Indian public holidays (see Annex A).  

## 3. Term  

The contract commences on **1 May 2025** and remains in effect for **24 months** (until 30 April 2027), unless terminated earlier per Clause 7.

## 4. Fees & Payment  

| Component | Rate | Frequency | Annual Cost |
|-----------|------|-----------|-------------|
| Fixed Platform Access | ₹1.20 crore | Yearly | ₹1.20 crore |
| Per‑Employee Processing | ₹1,200 per employee per month | Monthly | ₹2.64 crore (₹1,200 × 1,850 × 12) |

| GST (18 %) | Applied on total | Quarterly | ₹71.5 Lakh |

**Total annual payable amount:** **₹4.53 crore** (inclusive of GST).  

Invoices will be raised quarterly and payable within **30 days** of receipt. Late payment attracts interest at **1.5 % per month**.

## 5. Confidentiality & Data Security  

- Vendor shall treat all employee and payroll data as **Confidential Information** and shall not disclose it to any third party without prior written consent.  
- All data shall be stored on servers located in India, compliant with **ISO 27001** and **SOC 2 Type II** standards.  
- Data transmission shall be encrypted using **AES‑256** protocol.  

## 6. Compliance  

Vendor warrants that all services will comply with the **Income Tax Act 1961**, **Employees’ Provident Funds Act 1952**, **ESIC Act 1948**, and any other applicable Indian labour statutes.  

## 7. Termination  

- Either party may terminate with **60 days** written notice.  
- Immediate termination is permitted for material breach not cured within **15 days** of notice.  
- Upon termination, Vendor shall return or securely destroy all Client data within **10 business days**.

## 8. Dispute Resolution  

Any dispute shall be referred to **arbitration** under the **Indian Arbitration and Conciliation Act 1996**, with the seat in Bengaluru. The arbitrator’s award shall be final and binding.

## 9. Governing Law  

This contract shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the **laws of India**.
