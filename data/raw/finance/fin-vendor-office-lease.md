## 2. Premises  

The Lessor hereby leases to the Lessee the entire **5th Floor**, comprising **12,500 sq ft** of office space, together with common areas, lift, fire‑safety systems, and underground parking (30 car slots). The premises are located at **RMZ Ecoworld, HSR Layout, Bengaluru**.

## 3. Term  

- **Commencement Date:** 1 September 2024  
- **Initial Term:** 5 years, expiring on 31 August 2029  
- **Renewal Option:** Two (2) successive renewal terms of five (5) years each, upon written notice at least **90 days** prior to expiry.  

## 4. Rent & Payments  

| Component | Monthly Amount | Annual Total |
|-----------|----------------|--------------|
| Base Rent | ₹12.5 L | ₹1.5 crore |
| Service Charge (incl. cleaning, security) | ₹2.0 L | ₹24.0 L |
| **Total Monthly Payable** | **₹14.5 L** | **₹1.74 crore** |

- **Payment Schedule:** Rent payable in advance on or before the **5th** of each month via NEFT to the Lessor’s account (IFSC: SBIN0001234, Account No.: 001234567890).  
- **Annual Escalation:** 5 % increase on the base rent effective each anniversary of the Commencement Date.  
- **Late Payment Penalty:** 2 % per month on overdue amounts, compounded monthly.  

## 5. Security Deposit  

The Lessee shall deposit **₹3.0 crore** (equivalent to two (2) months’ total rent) within ten (10) days of signing. The amount will be held in a fixed‑deposit account and refunded, less any dues, within **30 days** after vacating the premises.

## 6. Maintenance & Utilities  

- **Structural & Major Repairs:** Lessor responsibility.  
- **Minor Repairs & Fit‑out Maintenance:** Lessee responsibility, subject to Lessor’s prior written approval.  
- **Utility Charges (electricity, water, telecom):** Billed to Lessee based on actual consumption.  
- **Annual Maintenance Fee:** ₹1.2 L payable on 1 January each year.

## 7. Tax & Regulatory  

All amounts are exclusive of **GST @ 18 %**, which shall be payable by the Lessee. The Lessee shall obtain and maintain all statutory approvals (e.g., fire‑NOC, municipal clearances) at its own cost.

## 8. Renewal & Escalation  

During any renewal term, the base rent shall be adjusted to the prevailing market rate, not to exceed **7 %** per annum, subject to mutual agreement and documented via a renewal addendum.

## 9. Termination  

- **Early Termination by Lessee:** Upon payment of **₹1.5 crore** (equivalent to six (6) months’ rent) as termination fee, plus settlement of all outstanding dues.  
- **Early Termination by Lessor:** Only for material breach, after **60 days** notice and opportunity to cure.  

## 10. Dispute Resolution  

Any dispute arising shall be referred to **arbitration** under the **Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996**, with the seat of arbitration in **Bengaluru**. The arbitral tribunal shall consist of a sole arbitrator mutually appointed by the parties.  

## 11. Governing Law  

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the **laws of India**, and the courts of **Bengaluru** shall have exclusive jurisdiction, except where arbitration is invoked.
