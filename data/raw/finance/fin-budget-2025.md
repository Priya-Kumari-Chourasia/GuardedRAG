# FY2025 Annual Budget

## Executive Summary  
The FY2025 budget for PKC Technologies (PKC) has been prepared by the Finance Department to support our strategic focus on AI‑driven SaaS platforms, expansion of the Austin satellite office, and continued investment in R&D. The projected total revenue for the year is **₹1,842.6 crore**, with an operating expense base of **₹1,527.3 crore**, delivering an expected EBITDA of **₹315.3 crore**. The budget aligns with the Board’s growth target of 22 % YoY revenue increase and a net profit margin of 12 %.

## Revenue Projections  

| Segment | FY2024 Actual | FY2025 Forecast | YoY Growth |
|---------|--------------|----------------|-----------|
| Enterprise AI Solutions (Bengaluru) | ₹1,012.4 crore | ₹1,235.9 crore | 22 % |
| Cloud SaaS Subscriptions (Global) | ₹512.7 crore | ₹588.3 crore | 15 % |
| Austin Office – US Market Services | ₹78.5 crore | ₹89.4 crore | 14 % |
| Professional Services & Training | ₹59.0 crore | ₹68.9 crore | 17 % |
| **Total Revenue** | **₹1,662.6 crore** | **₹1,842.6 crore** | **11 %** |

Revenue is driven by the rollout of the next‑gen “PKC Insight” platform (Q2 2025) and the renewal of three major enterprise contracts secured in FY2024.

## Expense Breakdown  

| Category | FY2025 Budget | % of Revenue |
|----------|--------------|--------------|
| Personnel Costs (incl. benefits) | ₹642.5 crore | 34.9 % |
| R&D (product development, labs) | ₹312.0 crore | 16.9 % |
| Sales & Marketing | ₹215.8 crore | 11.7 % |
| Cloud Infrastructure & Hosting | ₹124.6 crore | 6.8 % |
| General & Administrative (G&A) | ₹92.4 crore | 5.0 % |
| Travel & Entertainment (incl. Austin‑Bengaluru sync) | ₹28.0 crore | 1.5 % |
| Depreciation & Amortisation | ₹22.0 crore | 1.2 % |
| **Total Operating Expense** | **₹1,527.3 crore** | **82.9 %** |

Key expense highlights:  
- Incremental headcount of 120 engineers (Bengaluru) and 30 engineers (Austin) to support AI model scaling.  
- Marketing spend includes ₹45.0 L for the Diwali “Future of AI” roadshow (Nov 2025).  
- Cloud spend reflects a 20 % increase due to higher data‑processing volumes.

## Capital Expenditure (CapEx)  

- **Data Centre Expansion – Bengaluru:** ₹78.5 crore (completion by Dec 2025).  
- **Austin Office Renovation & Lab Setup:** ₹22.3 crore (targeted finish Q3 2025).  
- **Enterprise Security Suite Upgrade:** ₹15.0 crore (phased rollout).  

Total CapEx: **₹115.8 crore** (5.3 % of revenue).

## Staffing & Compensation  

- **Annual payroll processing dates:** 30 Jan (Republic Day bonus), 15 Aug (Independence Day bonus), 15 Oct (Diwali bonus).  
- **Average salary (Bengaluru):** ₹18.4 L per employee.  
- **Average salary (Austin):** ₹22.7 L (converted to INR).  
- **Key hires:**  
  - Dr. Ananya Rao – Head of AI Research (effective 01‑Apr‑2025, ₹42.7 crore package).  
  - Mr. Rajesh Iyer – US Operations Lead (effective 01‑Jun‑2025, ₹12.5 crore package).  

## Risk & Contingency  

- **Currency volatility:** 2 % contingency (₹36.9 crore) built into US‑related expenses.  
- **Regulatory compliance (data privacy):** ₹9.8 crore earmarked for legal counsel and audit.  
- **Talent attrition buffer:** ₹14.2 crore for retention bonuses.

## Key Assumptions  

1. Macro‑economic growth in India remains at 6 % YoY.  
2. No major changes to GST or corporate tax rates.  
3. Cloud service pricing remains stable; no significant cost‑inflation beyond 5 % YoY.
