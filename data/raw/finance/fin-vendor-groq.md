## 2. Scope of Services  

Vendor shall provide access to the Groq Cloud API platform for AI model inference and data processing, including:  

- Unlimited API calls for up to 10 TB of data transfer per month.  
- Dedicated support via email and a 24 × 7 helpline.  
- Quarterly performance review meetings (virtual) and annual on‑site health checks at PKC’s Bengaluru office.  

All services shall comply with the specifications attached as **Annex‑A**.

## 3. Term  

The Agreement commences on **01‑09‑2026** and shall remain in force for **24 months**, terminating automatically on **31‑08‑2028**, unless earlier terminated in accordance with Section 7.

## 4. Fees & Payment  

| Item | Rate | Frequency | Annual Cost |
|------|------|-----------|-------------|
| API Access (base) | ₹12.5 L per month | Monthly | ₹1.5 crore |
| Premium Support | ₹2.0 L per month | Monthly | ₹24 L |
| Over‑usage (beyond 10 TB) | ₹1,200 per GB | As incurred | Variable |

- **Invoice Schedule:** Vendor shall issue a consolidated invoice on the **5th** of each month.  
- **Payment Terms:** Net 30 days from invoice date. Payments falling on Indian public holidays (e.g., **26‑01‑2027 – Republic Day**) shall be due on the next business day.  
- **Late Payment Penalty:** 1.5 % per month on overdue amounts.  

All payments shall be made in Indian Rupees to Vendor’s bank account (HDFC Bank, IFSC HDFC0001234, A/C ₹9876543210).

## 5. Service Levels  

- **Uptime:** Minimum 99.7 % monthly uptime, measured on a calendar month basis.  
- **Response Time:** Critical tickets (P1) responded within 30 minutes; non‑critical (P3) within 4 hours.  
- **Remedies:** For each 0.1 % uptime shortfall, Vendor shall credit ₹1 L to the next invoice, capped at 10 % of monthly fees.

## 6. Confidentiality  

Both Parties shall treat all non‑public information as confidential and shall not disclose it to any third party without prior written consent, except as required by law. The confidentiality obligations survive termination for a period of **3 years**.

## 7. Termination  

- **For Cause:** Either Party may terminate with **30 days** written notice if the other Party materially breaches any term and fails to cure within that period.  
- **For Convenience:** Client may terminate after the first 12 months by providing **90 days** notice and paying a termination fee of **₹50 L**.  

Upon termination, Vendor shall cease processing PKC data within **10 business days** and return or destroy all confidential material.

## 8. Dispute Resolution  

Any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be referred to senior management for amicable settlement. Failing resolution, the dispute shall be arbitrated in Bengaluru under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, with a sole arbitrator appointed by the Indian Council of Arbitration. The award shall be final and binding.

## 9. Governing Law  

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of India.
