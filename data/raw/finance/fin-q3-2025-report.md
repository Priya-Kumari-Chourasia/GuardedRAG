# Q3 2025 Financial Report — ACTUALS

## Executive Summary  
The Q3 2025 financials for PKC Technologies (PKC) have been finalized as of 30 September 2025. Net revenue for the quarter stands at **₹42.7 crore**, surpassing the internal forecast of ₹39.3 crore by ₹3.4 crore (≈8.6%). The uplift is primarily attributable to accelerated enterprise contracts signed in August, notably with Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) and Reliance Industries, which together contributed an additional ₹2.1 crore over expectations.  

Overall, the quarter delivered an operating profit of **₹9.8 crore** and a net profit after tax of **₹7.2 crore**, reflecting disciplined cost management despite higher head‑count expenses.

> **Key take‑away:** Strong pipeline conversion and timely delivery of AI‑driven automation modules drove revenue beyond the forecast, reinforcing PKC’s market positioning in the Indian enterprise AI segment.

## Revenue Breakdown  

| Segment | Q3 2025 Actual | Q3 2024 | YoY Growth |
|---------|----------------|---------|------------|
| Enterprise AI Solutions | ₹28.5 crore | ₹22.1 crore | +28.9% |
| SaaS Subscriptions (monthly) | ₹9.3 crore | ₹8.4 crore | +10.7% |
| Professional Services | ₹4.9 crore | ₹5.2 crore | -5.8% |
| **Total Net Revenue** | **₹42.7 crore** | **₹35.7 crore** | **+19.6%** |

The enterprise segment accounted for 66.7% of total revenue, driven by three marquee deals closed on 12 August 2025 (TCS) and 23 August 2025 (Reliance). The SaaS line‑up grew organically through upsell of analytics dashboards to existing customers.

## Expense Summary  

- **Cost of Goods Sold (COGS):** ₹15.2 crore (35.6% of revenue)  
- **Research & Development:** ₹7.4 crore (17.3%) – includes ₹2.1 crore spent on the upcoming “PKC Vision” AI platform.  
- **Sales & Marketing:** ₹5.6 crore (13.1%) – higher spend on the “AI for Bharat” roadshow across Tier‑2 cities (Delhi, Hyderabad, Pune).  
- **General & Administrative:** ₹4.1 crore (9.6%) – includes ₹1.2 crore for the new Austin satellite office lease.  

Total operating expenses amounted to **₹32.3 crore**, yielding an operating margin of **23.0%**.

## Profitability  

- **EBITDA:** ₹11.5 crore  
- **Depreciation & Amortisation:** ₹1.7 crore  
- **Operating Profit:** ₹9.8 crore  
- **Finance Cost:** ₹0.6 crore (bank overdraft)  
- **Tax Expense (30%):** ₹2.0 crore  

**Net Profit after Tax:** **₹7.2 crore** (≈16.9% net margin).

## Cash Flow & Liquidity  

- **Operating Cash Flow:** ₹9.1 crore  
- **Investing Cash Flow:** –₹2.3 crore (capitalisation of R&D equipment)  
- **Financing Cash Flow:** –₹0.8 crore (repayment of short‑term loan)  

Closing cash balance as of 30 Sept 2025: **₹18.4 crore**. The company maintains a debt‑to‑equity ratio of 0.22, well within the board’s risk tolerance.

## Outlook & Guidance  

Given the robust Q3 performance, the finance team revises FY 2025 revenue guidance to **₹165 crore ± 3%**, up from the prior estimate of ₹158 crore. The forecast assumes:

- Continued enterprise conversions post‑Independence Day (15 Aug) and before the festive season.  
- Launch of “PKC Vision” in Q1 FY 2026, projected to add ₹6 crore in incremental SaaS revenue.  
- Controlled SG&A growth capped at 12% YoY.

Potential risks include macro‑economic slowdown and talent attrition in the AI talent pool. Mitigation measures involve a refreshed employee retention bonus program (effective 1 Oct 2025) and strategic hiring in the Austin hub.
