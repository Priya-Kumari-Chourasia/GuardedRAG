# Q2 2025 Financial Report

## Executive Summary  
Q2 2025 (1 Apr – 30 Jun 2025) marked a strong rebound for PKC Technologies, driven by the launch of **PKC‑Insight**, our AI‑driven analytics platform for the manufacturing sector. Consolidated revenue grew **28 % YoY** to **₹1,84.6 crore**, while operating profit improved to **₹32.8 crore**. Cash balances rose to **₹78.5 crore**, providing ample runway for the upcoming product expansion in the United States.

## Revenue Performance  

| Segment | Q2 2025 | Q2 2024 | YoY Δ |
|---------|---------|---------|-------|
| AI SaaS (subscription) | ₹78.2 crore | ₹61.5 crore | +27 % |
| Custom Solutions | ₹42.5 crore | ₹38.9 crore | +9 % |
| Licensing & Maintenance | ₹31.9 crore | ₹28.1 crore | +13 % |
| Professional Services | ₹12.0 crore | ₹10.1 crore | +18 % |
| **Total Revenue** | **₹1,84.6 crore** | **₹1,38.7 crore** | **+28 %** |

Key wins include a 3‑year contract with **Mahindra & Mahindra** (₹9.4 crore) and a strategic partnership with **Texas Instruments** for edge‑AI deployment in Austin.

## Expense Overview  

- **R&D:** ₹45.3 crore (24 % of revenue) – increased headcount in Bengaluru (30 new engineers) and Austin (12 new data scientists).  
- **SG&A:** ₹28.7 crore – marketing spend rose 15 % to support PKC‑Insight launch; travel expenses included a delegation to **Diwali 2025** (15 Oct) client roadshow.  
- **Cloud & Infrastructure:** ₹12.4 crore – migration of 40 % of workloads to a hybrid multi‑cloud model.  
- **Employee Benefits & Payroll:** ₹38.9 crore – total headcount 1,240 (Bengaluru 1,020; Austin 220).  

Total operating expenses: **₹1,25.3 crore**, yielding an **EBITDA margin of 31 %**.

## Profit & Loss  

- **Operating Profit:** ₹32.8 crore  
- **Finance Cost:** ₹1.2 crore (short‑term loan interest)  
- **Tax Expense (30 %):** ₹9.5 crore  
- **Net Profit:** **₹22.1 crore**  

## Cash Flow Highlights  

- **Operating Cash Flow:** ₹27.6 crore  
- **Investing Cash Flow:** –₹9.8 crore (CAPEX on new data centre in Whitefield)  
- **Financing Cash Flow:** –₹2.3 crore (partial repayment of term loan)  
- **Closing Cash & Cash Equivalents:** **₹78.5 crore**  

## Balance Sheet Snapshot (as of 30 Jun 2025)  

- **Total Assets:** ₹2,14.7 crore (Cash ₹78.5 crore, Fixed Assets ₹62.3 crore, Intangible Assets ₹34.9 crore)  
- **Total Liabilities:** ₹71.4 crore (Term Loan ₹45.0 crore, Trade Payables ₹26.4 crore)  
- **Equity:** ₹1,43.3 crore  

## Key Operational Metrics  

- **Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR):** ₹3,12.4 crore  
- **Customer Churn (Q2):** 3.2 % (down from 4.5 % YoY)  
- **Average Revenue per User (ARPU):** ₹2.8 lac  
- **Utilisation Rate – Custom Solutions:** 84 %  

## Outlook – Q3 2025  

- Target revenue **₹2,05 crore** (+11 % QoQ) with PKC‑Insight expected to contribute an additional **₹15 crore**.  
- Planned hiring of 50 engineers (Bengaluru) and 15 data scientists (Austin) to support the **‘Smart Factory’** roadmap.  
- Anticipated capital expenditure of **₹12.5 crore** for a new AI‑lab in Whitefield, scheduled for completion before **Diwali 2025** (15 Oct).
