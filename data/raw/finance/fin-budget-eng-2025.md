# Engineering Department Budget 2025

## 1. Executive Summary  

The Engineering Department budget for FY 2025 (1 Apr 2025 – 31 Mar 2026) is projected at **₹12.8 crore**. The plan supports the rollout of the next‑generation PKC‑AI Suite, expansion of the Bengaluru R&D hub, and the establishment of a dedicated AI‑Ops cell in Austin. The budget aligns with the corporate growth target of 25 % YoY revenue increase and incorporates a 10 % contingency for market volatility.

## 3. Capital Expenditure (CapEx)  

- **Server & GPU Farm (Bengaluru)** – ₹1.9 crore (procurement by 30 Jun 2025, installation by 15 Jul 2025).  
- **Edge Computing Nodes (Austin)** – ₹0.7 crore (deployment by 31 Oct 2025).  
- **AI Lab Renovation** – ₹0.6 crore (completion targeted before Republic Day, 26 Jan 2026).  
- **Software Licenses & Tools** – ₹0.45 crore (annual renewal on 1 Apr 2025).  

**Total CapEx:** **₹3.65 crore**

## 5. Revenue Projections  

- **PKC‑AI Suite v2.0** – Expected contracts: ₹5.5 crore (Q3 2025) and ₹4.0 crore (Q1 2026).  
- **Custom AI Solutions (Enterprise)** – ₹1.8 crore (spread across FY).  

**Total Projected Revenue:** **₹11.3 crore**  

## 6. Risk & Contingency  

A 10 % contingency fund of **₹1.28 crore** is earmarked for:  

- Currency fluctuation impact on Austin payroll.  
- Delays in hardware delivery due to global chip shortage.  
- Unforeseen regulatory compliance costs (e.g., post‑Independence Day audit, 15 Aug 2025).
