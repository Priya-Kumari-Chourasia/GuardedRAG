# FY2026 Annual Budget Draft

## Executive Summary  
The FY2026 Annual Budget Draft outlines PKC Technologies’ financial roadmap for the fiscal year 1 April 2026 – 31 March 2027. Projected total revenue stands at **₹1,245 crore**, driven by AI‑platform subscriptions and enterprise software services. Net profit after tax is targeted at **₹212 crore**. The budget aligns with our strategic pillars: product innovation, market expansion in North America, and talent acquisition.

## Revenue Projections  
| Segment | FY2025 Actual | FY2026 Forecast | YoY Growth |
|---------|---------------|----------------|-----------|
| AI SaaS (Bengaluru) | ₹452 crore | **₹518 crore** | 14.6 % |
| Enterprise Solutions (Austin) | ₹198 crore | **₹236 crore** | 19.2 % |
| Professional Services | ₹112 crore | **₹129 crore** | 15.2 % |
| Licensing & IP | ₹83 crore | **₹96 crore** | 15.7 % |
| **Total Revenue** | **₹845 crore** | **₹1,245 crore** | **47.3 %** |

Revenue assumptions incorporate a 10 % price uplift on SaaS contracts (effective 1 Oct 2026) and the onboarding of three new Fortune‑500 clients by Q2.

## Operating Expenses  
- **Personnel Costs:** ₹415 crore (incl. ₹85 crore for new hires)  
- **Employee Benefits & Welfare:** ₹62 crore  
- **Facility & Utilities (Bengaluru & Austin):** ₹48 crore  
- **Travel & Entertainment:** ₹22 crore (incl. business trips for Diwali & Republic Day client events)  
- **Legal & Compliance:** ₹11 crore  

**Total OPEX:** **₹558 crore** (44.8 % of revenue)

## Capital Expenditure (CapEx)  
- **Data Centre Expansion – Bengaluru:** ₹78 crore (completion by 30 Nov 2026)  
- **AI Research Lab – Austin:** ₹34 crore (phase‑1 rollout by 15 Jan 2027)  
- **Hardware Refresh (Servers, Networking):** ₹19 crore  
- **Software Licences & Tools:** ₹12 crore  

**Total CapEx:** **₹143 crore**

## Contingency & Risk Reserve  
A 3 % contingency reserve of **₹37 crore** is set aside to mitigate currency fluctuations, regulatory changes, and unforeseen operational disruptions.
