# Sales Department Budget 2025

## Executive Summary  
The Sales Department budget for FY 2025 (1 Apr 2025 – 31 Mar 2026) aligns with PKC Technologies’ corporate growth target of **₹1,250 crore** in total revenue. The sales function is allocated **₹215 crore** (≈ 17.2 % of total revenue) to drive new‑business acquisition, channel expansion, and customer retention across India and the United States. The budget reflects a 12 % YoY increase in headcount costs, a 9 % rise in travel & events, and a modest 4 % uplift in technology spend to support the rollout of the new AI‑Insights CRM platform.

## Revenue Forecast  
| Segment | FY 2024 Actual | FY 2025 Target | YoY Growth |
|---------|----------------|----------------|------------|
| Enterprise (B2B) – India | ₹620 crore | ₹680 crore | 9.7 % |
| Mid‑Market – India | ₹210 crore | ₹235 crore | 11.9 % |
| US Satellite Office (Austin) | ₹85 crore | ₹100 crore | 17.6 % |
| Services & Upsell | ₹55 crore | ₹60 crore | 9.1 % |
| **Total** | **₹970 crore** | **₹1,075 crore** | **10.9 %** |

The shortfall of **₹175 crore** versus the corporate target will be covered by the Product Innovation and Strategic Partnerships divisions.

## Travel, Events & Client Entertainment  
- **Domestic travel** (air, rail, accommodation): **₹4.2 crore**  
- **International travel (US & EU)**: **₹2.8 crore**  
- **Industry conferences** (e.g., NASSCOM AI Summit – 15 Feb 2025, TechCrunch Disrupt – 12 Oct 2025): **₹1.5 crore**  
- **Client entertainment** (Diwali gala – 4 Nov 2025, Republic Day lunch – 26 Jan 2026): **₹0.9 crore**  

## Marketing & Lead Generation  
- **Digital ad spend (LinkedIn, Google)**: **₹3.6 crore**  
- **Content & SEO**: **₹1.2 crore**  
- **Channel partner incentives**: **₹2.0 crore**  

## Technology & Tools  
- **AI‑Insights CRM license renewal**: **₹1.8 crore**  
- **Sales enablement platform (Seismic)**: **₹0.9 crore**  
- **Data analytics & BI tools**: **₹0.7 crore**  

## Capital Expenditure (CapEx)  
- **Upgrade of Bengaluru sales floor (ergonomic workstations, video‑conferencing pods)**: **₹1.1 crore**  
- **Austin office expansion (additional 2,000 sq ft)**: **₹0.8 crore**  

## Risk & Contingency  
A contingency reserve of **₹2.5 crore** (≈ 1 % of total budget) is set aside for currency fluctuation (USD/INR) and unexpected regulatory changes.
