# Marketing Budget Allocation 2025

## Overview  

PKC Technologies (PKC) aims to strengthen its market presence across India and the United States in FY 2025‑26. The Marketing Budget Allocation 2025 outlines a ₹12.5 crore plan, aligning with the corporate growth target of 25 % YoY revenue increase. The budget is approved by the Finance Committee on 12 January 2025 and will be executed from 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2026.

## Budget Summary  

| Category                     | FY 2025 Allocation | % of Total |
|------------------------------|-------------------|------------|
| Digital Advertising          | ₹4.2 crore        | 33.6 % |
| Events & Sponsorships        | ₹2.8 crore        | 22.4 % |
| Content & Creative Production| ₹1.9 crore        | 15.2 % |
| Public Relations & Media     | ₹1.5 crore        | 12.0 % |
| Market Research & Analytics  | ₹1.1 crore        | 8.8 % |
| Miscellaneous (contingency)  | ₹0.9 crore        | 7.2 % |
| **Total**                    | **₹12.5 crore**   | **100 %** |

## Allocation by Channel  

1. **Digital Advertising (₹4.2 crore)**  
   - Google Search & Display: ₹1.5 crore (Q1‑Q4)  
   - LinkedIn & Twitter Sponsored Posts: ₹1.0 crore (Q2‑Q4)  
   - Programmatic Video (YouTube, OTT): ₹1.2 crore (Q3‑Q4)  
   - Retargeting & CRM Automation: ₹0.5 crore (Q2‑Q4)  

2. **Events & Sponsorships (₹2.8 crore)**  
   - **India:** AI Expo Bengaluru (₹1.0 crore, 15 Oct 2025 – Republic Day week), TechSparks Delhi (₹0.6 crore, 5 Nov 2025), Diwali Community Hackathon (₹0.4 crore, 10 Dec 2025).  
   - **USA:** Austin AI Summit (₹0.5 crore, 20 Jan 2026), Startup Weekend Austin (₹0.3 crore, 12 Feb 2026).  

3. **Content & Creative Production (₹1.9 crore)**  
   - Video series “AI for Good” (₹0.8 crore, Q2‑Q3)  
   - White‑paper research (₹0.4 crore, Q1‑Q2)  
   - Regional language blogs & podcasts (₹0.5 crore, Q3‑Q4)  
   - Graphic design & brand refresh (₹0.2 crore, Q1).  

4. **Public Relations & Media (₹1.5 crore)**  
   - Press release distribution (₹0.6 crore)  
   - Media partnership with *The Economic Times* and *TechCrunch* (₹0.5 crore)  
   - Influencer collaborations (₹0.4 crore).  

5. **Market Research & Analytics (₹1.1 crore)**  
   - Customer segmentation study (₹0.5 crore)  
   - Competitive intelligence platform subscription (₹0.3 crore)  
   - Quarterly NPS & brand health surveys (₹0.3 crore).  

6. **Miscellaneous (₹0.9 crore)**  
   - Contingency reserve (₹0.5 crore)  
   - Legal & compliance for promotional activities (₹0.2 crore)  
   - Misc. travel & logistics (₹0.2 crore).

## Assumptions  

- Exchange rate stability (₹1 = USD 0.012) for any cross‑border spend.  
- No major regulatory changes affecting digital ad spend.  
- 10 % inflation on media rates YoY.
