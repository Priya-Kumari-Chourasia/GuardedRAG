# Annual Financial Summary 2024

## Executive Summary  
PKC Technologies delivered a robust FY‑2024 performance, posting a total revenue of **₹1,842.5 crore**, a **12.4%** YoY increase driven by strong uptake of our AI‑driven SaaS suite in the BFSI and e‑commerce segments. Net profit after tax stood at **₹212.7 crore**, reflecting disciplined cost management and a 9.1% margin expansion. The fiscal year closed with a cash balance of **₹378.9 crore** and a net debt of **₹45.3 crore**.

## Quarterly Highlights  

| Quarter | Revenue | YoY Growth | Op‑Ex (₹ crore) | Net Profit (₹ crore) |
|---------|---------|-----------|-----------------|----------------------|
| Q1 (Jan‑Mar) | ₹425.6L | 10.2% | 112.4 | 48.3 |
| Q2 (Apr‑Jun) | ₹452.1L | 13.5% | 118.7 | 52.9 |
| Q3 (Jul‑Sep) | ₹470.8L | 14.1% | 124.3 | 55.6 |
| Q4 (Oct‑Dec) | ₹493.9L | 12.8% | 130.5 | 55.9 |

*Note: Q4 revenue benefited from the Diwali promotional push (31 Oct 2024) and the launch of **PKC Insight 2.0** on 15 Nov 2024.*

## Revenue Breakdown  

- **AI SaaS Platforms** – ₹1,102.4 crore (59.9%)  
- **Custom AI Solutions** – ₹438.7 crore (23.8%)  
- **Professional Services & Training** – ₹301.4 crore (16.3%)  

Top clients include **State Bank of India**, **Reliance Retail**, and **Amazon India**. The Austin satellite office contributed **₹68.5 crore** (3.7%) primarily through US‑based cloud integration projects.

## Operating Expenses  

- **Personnel Costs** – ₹312.6 crore (including **₹45.2 crore** for new hires in Q3)  
- **R&D Investment** – ₹124.8 crore (6.8% of revenue)  
- **Sales & Marketing** – ₹98.3 crore (5.3% of revenue)  
- **Administrative & Overheads** – ₹62.1 crore  

The company observed a modest rise in travel expenses during **Republic Day** (26 Jan 2024) and **Independence Day** (15 Aug 2024) due to client visits in Delhi and Mumbai.

## Cash Flow & Capital Expenditure  

- **Operating Cash Flow** – ₹256.4 crore  
- **CapEx** – ₹84.7 crore (data‑center expansion in Bengaluru and edge‑node rollout in Hyderabad)  
- **Free Cash Flow** – ₹171.7 crore  

## Personnel & HR Updates  

- Total headcount: **1,842** (Bengaluru: 1,512; Austin: 330)  
- New hires in Q3: **112** (AI Researchers, Cloud Engineers)  
- Attrition rate: **4.2%** (below industry average)  

For payroll queries, contact **Anita Rao, HR Operations** at **+91‑80‑2345‑6789**.

## Outlook & Strategic Priorities (FY‑2025)  

1. **Scale AI SaaS** – target **₹2,150 crore** revenue (+16.7% YoY).  
2. **Expand US footprint** – open a new office in **San Jose** by Q2 2025.  
3. **Launch PKC Edge AI** – a low‑latency offering for IoT, slated for **15 Mar 2025**.  
4. **Sustain R&D spend at ≥7% of revenue** to maintain technology leadership.  

Management remains confident that the combination of product innovation, disciplined cost control, and strategic market expansion will deliver continued shareholder value.
