# Vendor Contract — AWS Enterprise Agreement

## 1. Overview  

This Vendor Contract formalises the **AWS Enterprise Agreement** between **PKC Technologies Pvt. Ltd.** (hereafter “PKC”) and **Amazon Web Services India Private Limited** (hereafter “AWS”). The agreement supersedes all prior informal arrangements and will govern the provision of cloud infrastructure, platform services, and related support for PKC’s AI‑driven product suite from **01‑04‑2025** to **31‑03‑2028**.

## 3. Scope of Services  

1. **Compute** – Provision of EC2, ECS, and Lambda resources for the **PKC Recommendation Engine** (launched Q2‑2025).  
2. **Storage & Database** – S3, EFS, Aurora, and DynamoDB for data lake and model artefacts.  
3. **Analytics & AI** – SageMaker, EMR, and Glue for model training and batch inference.  
4. **Support** – Enterprise‑Level Support (24×7 phone & chat) with a dedicated Technical Account Manager.  
5. **Security & Compliance** – Enablement of IAM, GuardDuty, and compliance reports aligned with ISO 27001, SOC 2, and Indian data‑localisation guidelines.

### Pricing Model  

- **On‑Demand** rates as per AWS India price list (effective 01‑04‑2025).  
- **Reserved Instances**: 3‑year term, 15 % discount, payable annually.  
- **Savings Plans**: Optional, to be reviewed annually.  

All amounts are exclusive of GST (₹18 %). PKC will remit GST‑inclusive invoices within **30 days** of receipt.

## 5. Billing & Payment

- Payments via NEFT/RTGS to PKC’s bank account (IFSC: **SBIN0001234**, Account No.: **001234567890**).  
- Late payment interest at **1.5 % per month** on overdue amounts.  

## 6. Term & Termination  

- **Effective Date:** 01‑04‑2025  
- **Initial Term:** 36 months, automatically renewing for successive 12‑month periods unless either party gives **90 days** written notice prior to expiry.  
- **Termination for Cause:** Either party may terminate with **30 days** notice if the other materially breaches any provision and fails to cure within that period.  

## 7. Governance & Reporting  

- Quarterly Business Review (QBR) on the **first Thursday of the month following each quarter** (e.g., 07‑01‑2026).  
- KPI Dashboard to include: compute utilisation (%), cost‑per‑model‑run, SLA adherence (≥ 99.9 % uptime).  
- Annual cost‑optimisation audit to be conducted by PKC’s Finance team in **January** (post‑Diwali).  

## 8. Compliance & Data Protection  

- AWS shall maintain **ISO 27001**, **SOC 2 Type II**, and **PCI‑DSS** certifications.  
- Data residency: All PKC workloads shall reside in the **ap‑south‑1 (Mumbai)** region unless expressly approved.  
- Incident response: AWS to notify PKC within **2 hours** of any security breach, in line with **Indian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT‑IN)** guidelines.
