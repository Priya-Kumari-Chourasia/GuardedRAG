# Q3 2025 Financial Report — FORECAST

## Executive Summary  
The Finance team presents the Q3 2025 revenue forecast for PKC Technologies (PKC). Based on current pipeline health, contract renewals, and anticipated new wins, we project **net revenue of approximately ₹38.5 crore** for the quarter ending 30 September 2025. This figure is a forward‑looking estimate and will be refined post‑quarter close.

## Key Revenue Drivers  

| Driver | Current Status (as of 12 Aug 2025) | Expected Impact |
|--------|-----------------------------------|-----------------|
| **Enterprise AI Suite (EAS)** – 3‑year renewal with Tata Motors | Signed on 5 Aug 2025, ₹7.2 crore annualised | +₹2.4 crore |
| **PKC Cloud Platform (PCP)** – New contract with State Bank of India | RFP awarded 22 Jul 2025, onboarding in Sep | +₹3.1 crore |
| **AI‑Powered Analytics for Healthcare** – Pilot with Apollo Hospitals | Pilot conversion expected by 30 Sep 2025 | +₹1.8 crore |
| **US Satellite Office Upsell** – Expansion with Dell Technologies | Proposal submitted 1 Sep 2025 | +₹0.9 crore (₹90 L) |
| **Seasonal Demand Spike** – Post‑Independence Day (15 Aug) marketing push | Historical Q3 uplift of 6 % YoY | +₹1.2 crore |

## Segment Breakdown (Projected)  

| Segment | Q3 2025 Forecast | YoY Growth |
|---------|------------------|------------|
| **Enterprise Solutions** | ₹22.4 crore | +12 % |
| **Cloud Services** | ₹9.6 crore | +15 % |
| **Healthcare AI** | ₹4.2 crore | +8 % |
| **US Market (Satellite)** | ₹2.3 crore | +10 % |
| **Other (Consulting, Training)** | ₹0.0 crore | – |

Total projected net revenue: **₹38.5 crore**.

## Core Assumptions  

1. **Currency Stability** – INR/USD exchange remains within ±2 % of the current rate (₹82/USD).  
2. **Retention Rate** – Enterprise client churn stays below 3 % quarterly.  
3. **Hiring** – No additional headcount beyond the approved Q3 budget of ₹1.5 crore for the Bengaluru R&D hub.  
4. **Regulatory** – No adverse changes in data‑privacy regulations affecting cloud deployments.  

## Risks & Mitigations  

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| Delay in PCP onboarding due to data‑center certification | Medium | ₹1.0 crore | Accelerate compliance audit; allocate a dedicated integration lead. |
| US market slowdown post‑Fed rate hike | Low | ₹0.5 crore | Diversify US pipeline; focus on existing contracts with upsell potential. |
| Talent attrition in AI research team | Medium | ₹0.8 crore | Implement retention bonus (₹5 L per senior researcher) and flexible work policy. |
