# Q1 2025 Financial Report

## Executive Summary  
PKC Technologies delivered a robust start to FY2025, posting **₹18.7 crore** total revenue in Q1 (Jan‑Mar 2025), a **12% YoY increase** driven by higher AI‑as‑a‑Service (AIaaS) contracts in the BFSI and e‑commerce sectors. Net profit stood at **₹3.2 crore**, reflecting disciplined cost management and a modest rise in R&D spend to fuel next‑gen product pipelines. The quarter closed with a cash balance of **₹9.5 crore**, comfortably above the company’s liquidity target of ₹7 crore.

## Revenue Breakdown  
| Segment | Q1 2025 | YoY Δ | Comments |
|---------|--------|------|----------|
| AIaaS (BFSI) | ₹7.4 crore | +15% | New 3‑year contract with State Bank of India (₹2.1 crore) signed on 12 Feb 2025 |
| AIaaS (E‑commerce) | ₹4.9 crore | +9% | Expansion of recommendation engine for FlipCart India |
| Custom Software Solutions | ₹3.6 crore | +6% | Delivery of ERP upgrade for a leading logistics firm |
| Licensing & Support | ₹2.8 crore | +8% | Renewals for PKC Vision Suite |

Total revenue: **₹18.7 crore** (₹17.0 crore Q1 2024).

## Expense Summary  
- **Cost of Services:** ₹6.1 crore (32% of revenue) – includes cloud hosting and third‑party API fees.  
- **Personnel Expenses:** ₹5.4 crore – salaries, statutory contributions, and performance bonuses for 210 employees (incl. 30 at the Austin satellite office).  
- **R&D Investment:** ₹2.2 crore – focus on generative AI models and edge‑AI deployment.  
- **Sales & Marketing:** ₹1.8 crore – participation in **Republic Day (26 Jan)** and **Mahashivratri (8 Mar)** corporate outreach events.  
- **Administrative & Other:** ₹1.0 crore – office rent (Bengaluru: ₹1.2 crore annualized; Austin: ₹0.6 crore), utilities, and legal fees.

Total expenses: **₹16.5 crore**, yielding an operating profit of **₹2.2 crore**.

## Profit & Loss Highlights  
- **EBITDA:** ₹3.0 crore  
- **Depreciation & Amortisation:** ₹0.5 crore  
- **Net Profit (after tax @ 25%):** **₹3.2 crore**  
- **Profit Margin:** 17.1% (up from 15.4% in Q1 2024)

## Cash Flow & Balance Sheet Snapshot  
- **Operating Cash Flow:** ₹2.8 crore  
- **Investing Cash Flow:** –₹0.9 crore (capitalisation of new AI lab equipment)  
- **Financing Cash Flow:** ₹0.0 crore (no new debt or equity in the quarter)  
- **Cash & Cash Equivalents (31 Mar 2025):** **₹9.5 crore**  
- **Total Debt:** ₹1.2 crore (short‑term working capital facility)  

## Key Operational Metrics  
- **Active Users (AIaaS platform):** 1.84 million (↑ 11% QoQ)  
- **Average Revenue Per User (ARPU):** ₹1,020  
- **Employee Utilisation Rate:** 78%  
- **Customer Churn:** 3.2% (target ≤ 4%)  

## Outlook & Action Plan (Q2 2025)  
1. **Launch PKC EdgeAI Suite** – targeted for 15 May 2025, expected to add ₹2.5 crore incremental revenue.  
2. **Expand US footprint** – hiring of 12 senior engineers in Austin; budgeted spend ₹1.1 crore.  
3. **Cost Optimisation** – aim to reduce cloud hosting spend by 5% through multi‑cloud negotiation.  
4. **Compliance & Governance** – complete ISO 27001 certification by 30 Jun 2025.
