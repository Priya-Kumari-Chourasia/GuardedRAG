# PKC Holiday Calendar 2026

## Overview  

PKC Technologies observes a unified holiday calendar for all employees, with additional regional observances for the Bengaluru headquarters and the Austin satellite office. The calendar balances national statutory holidays, cultural celebrations, and operational downtime to ensure business continuity while respecting employee well‑being.  

**Key points**  

- **Total statutory holidays (India):** 16 days  
- **Additional Bengaluru observances:** 2 days (Karnataka Rajyotsava & Bengaluru Tech Fest)  
- **Austin office statutory holidays (USA):** 10 days (federal holidays)  
- **Floating holidays per employee:** 2 days (chosen by the employee)  
- **Holiday allowance budget:** ₹12.5L per employee for the FY‑2026 (covers travel, gifts, and wellness activities)  

All offices will be fully closed on the dates listed below. Critical support teams will operate on a 24‑hour on‑call rotation with a modest on‑call premium of ₹1.8L per employee for the year.

## Floating & Optional Holidays  

- Each employee may select **two floating days** from the list of non‑working days (e.g., personal birthdays, regional festivals).  
- The HR portal will open on **1 March 2026** for selections; changes are allowed until **30 June 2026**.  

---  

## Holiday Allowance & Policy  

- **Annual holiday allowance:** ₹12.5L per employee (covers travel, gifts, wellness).  
- **Holiday party budget:** ₹3.2 crore for the FY‑2026 (includes Bengaluru year‑end gala and Austin winter celebration).  
- Unused allowance rolls over **once** to the next fiscal year, capped at ₹2.0L.
