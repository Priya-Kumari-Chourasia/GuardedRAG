# PKC Employee Handbook 2026

## Welcome to PKC Technologies  

Welcome aboard! PKC Technologies (PKC) is a fast‑growing AI and software solutions provider headquartered in Bengaluru, with a satellite office in Austin, USA. Our FY 2025 revenue was ₹850 crore and we are targeting ₹950 crore for FY 2026. This handbook outlines the policies, benefits, and expectations that help us deliver world‑class products while fostering a supportive workplace.

## Company Overview  

- **Founded:** 2014  
- **Headquarters:** 12th Floor, Prestige Tech Park, Whitefield, Bengaluru – +91‑80‑1234‑5678  
- **Satellite Office:** 300 W. 24th St., Austin, TX – +1‑512‑555‑0123  
- **Employees (2026):** 1,240 (Bengaluru 1,050; Austin 190)  
- **Core Services:** AI‑driven analytics, enterprise SaaS, custom automation, and cloud migration.

| Category | Details |

## Compensation & Benefits  

- **Salary Bands (Annual Gross)**  
  - Junior Developer: ₹6.5 L – ₹9 L  
  - Senior Developer: ₹15 L – ₹22 L  
  - Lead Engineer: ₹28 L – ₹38 L  
  - Manager: ₹45 L – ₹60 L  

- **Variable Pay** – Annual performance bonus up to 20 % of base salary, linked to company targets (e.g., FY 2026 bonus pool ₹45 crore).  

- **Statutory Benefits** – EPF (12 % of basic), Gratuity (₹15 L cap), Employee State Insurance (ESI).  

- **Health & Wellness** – Medical insurance covering up to ₹1.5 L per employee per year; family coverage up to ₹3 L. Annual health check‑up and mental‑wellness stipend of ₹12 k.  

- **Retirement & Savings** – Company‑matched NPS contribution up to 4 % of basic salary.  

- **Leave Entitlement**  
  - Earned Leave: 18 days/year (accrues monthly).  
  - Sick Leave: 12 days/year (medical certificate required after 2 days).  
  - Maternity/Paternity: 26 weeks (maternity) / 15 days (paternity).

## Code of Conduct  

1. **Integrity** – No conflict of interest, gifts over ₹5 k must be disclosed.  
2. **Confidentiality** – All client data and PKC IP are classified as “Confidential”. Breaches are subject to disciplinary action.  
3. **Harassment Policy** – Zero tolerance. Report any incident to HR via the confidential portal or call +91‑80‑1234‑5678.  

## Performance Management  

- **Quarterly Reviews** – Objective‑based scoring (KPIs, OKRs).  
- **Annual Appraisal** – Salary revision (average 8 % increase) and promotion eligibility.  
- **Learning & Development** – Annual training budget of ₹30 k per employee; access to PKC Academy (online courses, certifications).
