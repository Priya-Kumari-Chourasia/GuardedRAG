# Product Roadmap H1 2026

## Executive Summary  
PKC Technologies aims to solidify its position as the leading AI‑driven automation platform for enterprise customers in H1 2026. The roadmap focuses on three strategic pillars: **Intelligent Process Automation (IPA) 2.0**, **Conversational AI Suite**, and **Edge‑AI for IoT**. By the end of June 2026 we target a cumulative ARR of **₹12.5 crore**, up from **₹9.8 crore** in FY 2025, and a net‑profit margin of **18 %**.

## Feature Releases  

- **Adaptive Workflow Engine** – AI‑driven decision nodes, drag‑and‑drop UI, integration with SAP & Oracle.  
- **Multilingual NLU** – Support for 12 Indian languages, real‑time transliteration.  
- **Edge‑AI Optimizer** – Model quantization for ARM Cortex‑M, on‑device inference < 30 ms.  
- **Analytics Dashboard 2.0** – Predictive KPI alerts, export to PowerBI & Tableau.

- **Projected Revenue H1 2026:** **₹12.5 crore** (↑ 27 % YoY).
