# Company Update: Strategic Announcement (All-Hands Summary)

## Overview  

During today’s all‑hands, the leadership team announced a strategic shift that will position PKC Technologies for the next phase of growth. Over the past fiscal year we have expanded our AI platform footprint across ₹150 crore in revenue and increased our headcount to 1,200 employees, with a balanced presence in Bengaluru and Austin. The new direction builds on this momentum, focusing on deeper industry vertical integration and accelerated product innovation.

## Strategic Direction  

1. **Vertical‑Focused AI Solutions** – We will prioritize tailored AI offerings for healthcare, fintech, and manufacturing, leveraging our core technology stack to deliver faster time‑to‑value for clients.  
2. **Platform Expansion** – A major upgrade to the PKC Cloud Suite is slated for Q1 FY 2025, introducing low‑code capabilities and enhanced data governance.  
3. **Global Collaboration** – The Austin satellite office will serve as a hub for North‑American client engagement and joint R&D, complementing our Bengaluru engineering excellence.  

These initiatives are expected to drive a sustainable growth trajectory, with projected revenue growth of **₹20‑30 crore** YoY and an increase in operating efficiency.

## Impact on Employees  

- **Compensation & Benefits** – The average total compensation package will rise to approximately **₹12 L** per annum, reflecting market competitiveness and our commitment to rewarding talent.  
- **Learning & Development** – A new internal academy will launch in November, offering up to **₹5 L** in upskilling credits per employee per year.  
- **Work‑Life Balance** – We are introducing flexible hybrid work policies, with core office days on Tuesdays and Thursdays. All Indian holidays, including Diwali (15 Nov 2025) and Pongal (14 Jan 2025), will continue to be observed.  

No immediate changes to reporting structures are anticipated; the focus remains on empowering every team to innovate within the new strategic framework.

## Frequently Asked Questions  

- **Will there be any layoffs?**  
  No. The announcement is about growth and capability expansion, not workforce reduction.  

- **How will my role be affected?**  
  Your current responsibilities remain unchanged. Future projects will align with the vertical focus, offering new opportunities for impact.  

- **What support is available for upskilling?**  
  The internal academy will provide curated learning paths, mentorship, and certification subsidies.
