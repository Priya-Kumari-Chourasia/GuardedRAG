# Employee Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Purpose  
The Employee Expense Reimbursement Policy (the “Policy”) establishes clear guidelines for the submission, approval, and reimbursement of legitimate business expenses incurred by PKC Technologies employees. It ensures compliance with Indian tax regulations, promotes fiscal responsibility, and provides a transparent process for all staff across the Bengaluru headquarters and the Austin satellite office.

## Scope  
Applies to all full‑time, part‑time, contractual, and intern staff of PKC Technologies worldwide. The Policy covers travel, accommodation, meals, communication, and other approved business expenditures incurred in the performance of official duties.

## Expense Categories & Limits  

| Category | Maximum Allowable Rate (per occurrence) | Remarks |
|----------|------------------------------------------|---------|
| Domestic Air (Economy) | ₹12.5L per annum (capped at ₹1.2L per ticket) | Must be booked through PKC’s travel portal. |
| International Air (Economy) | ₹42.7 crore per annum (capped at ₹3.5L per ticket) | Only for approved conferences or client visits. |
| Hotel (Standard) | ₹15,000 per night (Bengaluru) / ₹12,000 per night (other Indian cities) | 4‑star or equivalent; no upgrades without prior approval. |
| Meals (Per Diem) | ₹2,500 per day (incl. GST) | Covers breakfast, lunch, dinner, and incidental items. |
| Local Transport (Taxi/Auto) | ₹1,200 per day | Use OLA/Uber corporate accounts where possible. |
| Internet & Phone | ₹3,000 per month (reimbursement of actual bill) | Must be a business‑related expense. |
| Conference Fees | Up to ₹5L per event | Requires pre‑approval from the Department Head. |

## Eligibility & Documentation  

1. **Pre‑Approval** – All travel and conference expenses must be approved via the PKC expense portal (e‑expense.pkctech.in) at least 5 business days before the planned activity.  
2. **Original Receipts** – Submit scanned copies of GST‑compliant invoices/receipts. Handwritten notes are not accepted.  
3. **Expense Report Deadline** – Claims must be filed within **30 days** of the expense date. Late submissions may be denied.  
4. **Currency Conversion** – For international expenses, use the RBI’s published exchange rate on the date of transaction.  

## Reimbursement Process  

1. Log in to the expense portal and create a new claim.  
2. Attach all required receipts and a brief justification (max 150 characters).  
3. The immediate manager reviews and approves within **3 business days**.  
4. Finance validates tax compliance and processes payment within **7 business days** of final approval.  
5. Reimbursements are credited to the employee’s bank account via NEFT; a confirmation email is sent from finance@pkctech.in.  

## Non‑Reimbursable Items  

- Personal entertainment (movies, concerts)  
- Alcoholic beverages  
- Fines, penalties, or traffic violations  
- Expenses incurred on Indian public holidays (e.g., Republic Day – 26‑01‑2024, Diwali – 15‑11‑2024) unless pre‑approved for critical business needs.  

## Roles & Responsibilities  

- **Employee** – Submit accurate claims with proper documentation.  
- **Manager** – Verify business relevance and approve within stipulated timelines.  
- **Finance Team** – Ensure compliance with GST and income‑tax regulations; address queries at +91‑80‑1234‑5678.  
- **HR & Compliance** – Conduct annual audits and update policy as required.  

## Policy Review  
The Policy will be reviewed annually on **01‑04‑2025** and updated to reflect regulatory changes or operational needs. All revisions will be communicated via the internal portal and email.
