# Product Roadmap H2 2025

## Overview  
PKC Technologies (PKC) will focus H2 2025 on expanding its AI‑driven enterprise suite and deepening market penetration in the APAC and North‑America verticals. The roadmap aligns with FY25‑26 revenue guidance of **₹1,240 crore** and a projected net‑new ARR of **₹210 crore** from the upcoming releases.

## Strategic Objectives  

| Objective | Target | Owner |
|-----------|--------|-------|
| Grow enterprise AI‑adoption in Banking & FinTech | **₹85 crore** incremental ARR | Ramesh Kumar (Head‑of‑Enterprise) |
| Launch two new SaaS modules (SecureEdge 2.0, DataPulse Insights) | **₹45 crore** ARR by FY26 | Priya Sharma (Product Lead) |
| Reduce time‑to‑market for feature releases from 10 weeks to 6 weeks | 40 % improvement | Amit Verma (Engineering) |
| Achieve a gross margin of **68 %** on AI‑Assist Pro | FY25‑26 | Nisha Patel (Finance) |

## Key Initiatives  

1. **AI‑Assist Pro v3** – AI‑augmented coding assistant for developers.  
   - Release date: **15 Oct 2025** (Gandhi Jayanti).  
   - Investment: **₹12.5 crore** (R&D, cloud spend).  

2. **SecureEdge 2.0** – Zero‑trust security platform for hybrid clouds.  
   - Beta launch: **30 Sep 2025** (post‑Monsoon break).  
   - Full launch: **20 Nov 2025**.  
   - Spend: **₹9.8 crore** (security infra, compliance).  

3. **DataPulse Insights** – Real‑time analytics dashboard for supply‑chain optimization.  
   - Pilot with three logistics partners: **1 Dec 2025**.  
   - Commercial rollout: **15 Jan 2026**.  
   - Budget: **₹7.2 crore**.  

4. **Austin Satellite Hub Expansion** – Add a dedicated AI‑Ops team (12 engineers).  
   - Hiring budget: **₹2.4 crore** (annual salaries).  
   - Office fit‑out: **₹1.1 crore**.

## Resource Allocation  

- **R&D Headcount:** 78 engineers (Bengaluru) + 12 (Austin).  
- **Annual Salary Outlay:** **₹185 crore** (average **₹23.5L** per senior engineer).  
- **Operating Expenditure:** Cloud services **₹28 crore**, Marketing **₹15 crore**, Travel & client‑engagement **₹4.2 crore** (including inbound trips to Delhi & Mumbai).  

## Risks & Mitigation  

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| Cloud‑cost volatility | ↑ OPEX by **₹3 crore** | Negotiate tiered pricing, implement auto‑scaling policies |
| Talent attrition in AI‑Ops | Project delays | Introduce retention bonus **₹3 L** per engineer, mentorship program |
| Regulatory changes (Data‑Privacy) | Feature hold‑up | Early legal review, engage KPMG India for compliance audit |

## KPI & Success Metrics  

- **ARR Growth:** +17 % YoY (target **₹210 crore**).  
- **Feature Cycle Time:** ≤ 6 weeks (tracked via Jira).  
- **Customer NPS:** ≥ 68 (post‑launch surveys).  
- **Utilisation Rate:** ≥ 85 % of cloud capacity (CloudHealth dashboard).
