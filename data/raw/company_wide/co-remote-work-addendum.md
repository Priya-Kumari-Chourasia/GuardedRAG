# Remote Work Policy Addendum

## 1. Purpose  
The Remote Work Policy Addendum (the “Addendum”) supplements PKC Technologies’ existing Work‑From‑Home (WFH) framework. It clarifies eligibility, financial entitlements, security expectations, and compliance checkpoints for employees who work remotely from locations outside the Bengaluru headquarters or the Austin satellite office. The Addendum is effective **1 January 2025** and will be reviewed annually.

## 2. Scope  
Applies to all full‑time staff (permanent, contract‑to‑hire, and interns) across product, engineering, sales, support, and corporate functions. Part‑time consultants and third‑party vendors are excluded unless expressly authorised by the Head of HR.

## 3. Eligibility & Approval Process  
| Role | Minimum tenure | Approval chain |
|------|----------------|----------------|
| Senior Engineer, Manager, Director | 12 months | Reporting Manager → HR Business Partner → CFO |
| Junior Engineer, Analyst, Associate | 6 months | Reporting Manager → HR Business Partner |
| Intern (≤ 6 months) | N/A | Reporting Manager → HR Business Partner |

Requests must be submitted via the internal portal **PKC‑WFH‑2025** no later than **15 days** before the intended start date. Approvals are valid for a maximum of **180 days** and are renewable upon re‑evaluation.

## 4. Financial Stipends & Allowances  
- **Home‑Office Stipend:** ₹5,000 per month (taxable) to cover internet, electricity, and ergonomic accessories.  
- **Equipment Budget:** One‑time allocation of up to **₹1.5 L** for laptop, docking station, monitor(s), and headset. Unused funds must be returned at the end of the fiscal year.  
- **Travel Reimbursement:** For mandatory on‑site meetings, mileage is reimbursed at ₹8 per km, capped at **₹30,000** per annum.

## 5. Equipment & Connectivity  
All remote workstations must be PKC‑issued hardware. Employees may procure peripherals within the equipment budget but must submit receipts to the IT Asset Register. Minimum broadband speed: **25 Mbps** download, **5 Mbps** upload. Employees are responsible for maintaining a secure Wi‑Fi password and must use PKC’s VPN (PKC‑VPN‑2025) for all corporate traffic.

## 6. Security & Data Protection  
- Devices must have **BitLocker** (Windows) or **FileVault** (macOS) enabled.  
- Confidential data must be stored only on PKC‑approved cloud drives (PKC‑OneDrive).  
- Any security incident must be reported within **4 hours** to the Security Operations Centre (SOC) at **+91‑80‑1234‑5678**.

## 7. Communication & Availability  
Remote employees must be reachable during core hours **10:00 – 16:00 IST** (or **09:00 – 15:00 CST** for Austin staff). Daily check‑ins are conducted via Teams at **11:00 IST**. Annual leave and Indian public holidays (e.g., **Republic Day 26 Jan**, **Independence Day 15 Aug**, **Diwali 15 Nov 2025**) are observed as per the Company Calendar.

## 8. Performance & Monitoring  
Quarterly performance reviews will incorporate remote‑work KPIs: task completion rate, response time, and collaboration metrics. Managers may request screen‑share sessions for audit purposes, with a 24‑hour notice.

## 9. Compliance & Disciplinary Action  
Non‑adherence to the Addendum (e.g., misuse of stipend, breach of security protocols) may result in **₹10,000** warning fines, revocation of remote privileges, or termination per the Employee Handbook.

## 10. Review & Amendments  
The HR and Finance heads will jointly review the Addendum on **1 July 2025**. Amendments require sign‑off from the CEO and will be communicated via the PKC Intranet.
