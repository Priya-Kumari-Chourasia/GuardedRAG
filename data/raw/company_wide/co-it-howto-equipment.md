# IT How-To: Requesting New Equipment

## Purpose  
This How‑To guide explains the end‑to‑end process for PKC Technologies employees to request new hardware or peripheral equipment (laptops, monitors, servers, networking gear, etc.). Following the steps ensures compliance with the ₹2 crore annual equipment cap, aligns with the FY 2025‑26 budget, and speeds up approvals.

## Scope  
Applicable to all staff at the Bengaluru headquarters and the Austin satellite office. The procedure covers requests for personal workstations, team‑level devices, and data‑center hardware.

## Eligibility & Budget Limits  

| Category | Maximum per request | Annual departmental cap |
|----------|--------------------|--------------------------|
| Individual workstation (laptop/monitor) | ₹1.5 L | ₹30 L |
| Team‑level peripherals (dock, headset, printer) | ₹2 L | ₹50 L |
| Server / networking hardware | ₹5 L | ₹2 crore |

Requests exceeding the per‑item limit must be split into multiple submissions or routed through the **Strategic Procurement Board**.

## Step‑by‑Step Process  

1. **Identify Need**  
   - Verify that the required equipment is not already available in the shared pool (check the Asset Management Portal – PKC‑AMS).  
   - Confirm the justification aligns with the employee’s role and project deliverables (e.g., “AI model training on GPU‑enabled workstation for Project Aquila”).

2. **Complete the Equipment Request Form (ERF‑2025)**  
   - Download the latest ERF from the **IT Service Hub** (link: `https://itsupport.pkctechnologies.in/forms/ERF-2025`).  
   - Fill in:  
     - Employee name, ID, and contact (mobile: +91‑80‑1234 5678).  
     - Department head’s email (e.g., `ramesh.kumar@pkctechnologies.in`).  
     - Detailed specification, vendor quote, and total cost (use INR notation, e.g., ₹1.28 L).  
   - Attach up to three vendor quotations; at least one must be from an approved vendor list.

3. **Managerial Approval**  
   - Forward the completed ERF to your reporting manager.  
   - Manager adds a brief endorsement and forwards to **Finance – Capital Expenditure (CapEx)** within **3 business days**.  

4. **Finance Review**  
   - Finance validates budget availability against the departmental cap.  
   - If the request falls within the **₹30 L** individual limit, Finance signs off and forwards to **IT Procurement**.  
   - For amounts > ₹5 L, Finance escalates to the **Strategic Procurement Board** (meeting scheduled quarterly; next meeting: 15‑01‑2026, post‑Republic Day).

5. **IT Procurement Processing**  
   - Procurement issues a Purchase Order (PO) and notifies the vendor.  
   - Expected delivery timeline: **7‑10 working days** for standard items; **15‑20 days** for custom hardware.  

6. **Asset Registration & Deployment**  
   - Upon receipt, the IT Asset Team logs the equipment in PKC‑AMS, tags it with an asset ID, and schedules a hand‑over.  
   - The employee signs the **Equipment Acceptance Form** (EAF‑2025) and returns a scanned copy to IT.

7. **Record Keeping**  
   - All documents (ERF, approvals, PO, EAF) are stored in the **Document Management System** under folder `IT/EquipmentRequests/2025`.  
   - Retention period: **5 years** as per PKC Records Policy.

## Important Dates & Holidays  

- **Diwali** (15‑11‑2025) – IT Service Desk will operate on **limited support**; submit urgent requests by **10‑Nov‑2025**.  
- **Republic Day** (26‑01‑2026) – No approvals processed on this day; plan accordingly.
