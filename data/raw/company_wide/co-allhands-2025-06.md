# All-Hands Notes — June 2025

- **CEO Ananya Rao** opened the meeting with a quick recap of Q2 performance and a thank‑you to the Bengaluru and Austin teams for hitting the **₹12.5 crore** revenue target two weeks ahead of schedule.

## Product Updates  

- **SmartScale** (AI‑driven auto‑scaling for cloud workloads) entered GA on **12 June**. Early adopters report a **30 %** reduction in cloud spend.  
- **VisionX** (computer‑vision SDK) moved to beta on **3 June**; the pilot with a major retail chain in Delhi is slated for a full rollout in Q3.  
- **Compliance Suite** received ISO 27001 certification on **28 May** – a key requirement for our upcoming government contracts.
