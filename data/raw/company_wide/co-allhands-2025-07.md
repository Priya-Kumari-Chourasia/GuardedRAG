# All-Hands Notes — July 2025

## Company Overview  

July 2025 was a milestone month for PKC Technologies. Our Bengaluru headquarters celebrated its 8‑year anniversary, and the Austin satellite office marked its 2‑year milestone with a virtual “Founders’ Day” on 12 July. Across both locations, we now have **1,240** employees (Bengaluru — 1,020; Austin — 220).  

## Financial Highlights  

- **Revenue:** ₹12.6 crore for Q2 FY25, a 22 % YoY increase driven by the launch of PKC‑Assist v2.0.  
- **Operating Expenses:** ₹8.9 crore, with R&D accounting for ₹3.4 crore (38 % of total spend).  
- **Net Profit:** ₹3.2 crore, up from ₹2.1 crore in Q2 FY24.  
- **Cash Position:** ₹5.7 crore in the corporate account, comfortably above our 12‑month runway target.  

## Product Updates  

| Product | Release | Key Metrics (July) |
|---------|---------|--------------------|
| **PKC‑Assist v2.0** | 5 July | 4.1 L active users, 96 % satisfaction score, ₹1.8 crore ARR |
| **VisionAI Suite** | 19 July | 1.2 L enterprise contracts, ₹2.3 crore ARR |
| **PKC‑Edge** (IoT) | 28 July | Pilot with 3 major manufacturers, projected ₹0.9 crore ARR in FY26 |

The AI‑driven analytics module now supports 12 Indian languages, a direct response to feedback from our regional sales teams.

## People & Culture  

- **New Hires:** 45 engineers (₹12 L per annum avg.), 12 sales executives (₹9 L per annum avg.), and 8 support staff.  
- **Promotions:** Priya Nair (Senior Product Manager → Group Product Lead), Arjun Rao (Lead Data Scientist → Head of R&D).
