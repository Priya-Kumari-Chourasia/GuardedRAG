# Office Closure Notice — Diwali 2025

## Overview  

PKC Technologies (PKC) will observe a company‑wide shutdown in conjunction with the Diwali festivities in 2025. The closure aligns with the national holiday calendar and supports our employees’ ability to celebrate with their families. All operational units – Bengaluru headquarters, Austin satellite office, and remote teams – will follow the same schedule.

## Closure Dates  

| Date (DD‑MM‑YYYY) | Day | Status |
|-------------------|-----|--------|
| 24‑10‑2025 | Friday | Regular working day (last working day before holiday) |
| 25‑10‑2025 | Saturday | Diwali (Deepavali) – Company holiday |
| 26‑10‑2025 | Sunday | Govardhan Puja – Company holiday |
| 27‑10‑2025 | Monday | Bhai Dooj – Company holiday |
| 28‑10‑2025 | Tuesday | Normal working day (operations resume) |

All client‑facing services, internal support desks, and development pipelines will be paused from 00:01 hrs on 25‑10‑2025 and will recommence at 09:00 hrs on 28‑10‑2025 (IST). The Austin office will observe the same dates, adjusting for the local time zone (UTC‑6).

## Payroll & Benefits  

- **Diwali Bonus:** Each full‑time employee will receive a one‑time Diwali bonus of **₹1.5 L** (₹150,000). The total outlay for the bonus program is projected at **₹180 crore**, based on our current headcount of 1,200 staff.  
- **Leave Balance:** Employees may carry forward up to **5 days** of earned leave into the next fiscal year. Any unused leave beyond this limit will be encashed at the standard rate of **₹2,500 per day**.  
- **Expense Reimbursement:** Travel or relocation expenses incurred for Diwali‑related family visits will be reimbursed up to **₹25 k** per employee, subject to submission of valid receipts by 15‑11‑2025.

## Operations Impact  

- **Client Deliverables:** Project managers must ensure that all critical milestones slated for the week of 20‑10‑2025 are either completed or have a clear hand‑over plan by 24‑10‑2025.  
- **Support Desk:** Tier‑1 support tickets will be auto‑responded with a holiday notice. Tier‑2 and Tier‑3 escalations will be monitored on a rotating on‑call basis; the on‑call roster will be shared by 10‑10‑2025.  
- **Infrastructure:** Data centre maintenance windows scheduled for 26‑10‑2025 are postponed to 02‑11‑2025 to avoid service disruption.
